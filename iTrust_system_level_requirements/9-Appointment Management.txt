The LHCP schedules an appointment by entering the patient's MID, selecting the appointment type, and specifying the date/time (which must be in the future). A comment (optional) can be added. Both patients and LHCPs can view their upcoming appointments, sorted by date/time, with conflicts highlighted. The user can click to read appointment comments. LHCPs can also view recent lab results, see notifications for the day's appointments, and manage report acceptances or rejections. Patients only have access to view their upcoming appointments.
