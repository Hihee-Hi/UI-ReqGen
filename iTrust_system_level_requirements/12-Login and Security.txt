Users can change their password, with email notifications sent for successful changes. After three failed login attempts, access is blocked from the IP address for 15 minutes. If no security question is set, users must use their random password; if set, they must answer the question correctly to change their password. Sessions terminate after a configurable period of inactivity, and all actions (like login failures or data modifications) are logged with relevant details, including user IDs, transaction types, and timestamps.
