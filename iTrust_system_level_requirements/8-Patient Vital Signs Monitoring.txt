In the iTrust system, patients, UAPs, and personal representatives can report blood pressure and glucose levels. If the values are outside the allowed ranges (systolic BP 40-240, diastolic BP 40-150, glucose 0-250), the user is prompted to correct the data. If more than ten data points are entered in one day, the system restricts further input.

LHCPs and UAPs can add or remove patients from the monitoring list and specify which physiological data to monitor. Patients and their representatives can report data, which is saved with a timestamp and status (self-reported, reported by UAP, or reported by a representative).

LHCPs can view monitoring data for all their patients, with out-of-range values highlighted. UAPs and patient representatives can report data only for patients they are authorized to monitor, with applicable fields displayed based on the monitoring settings.
