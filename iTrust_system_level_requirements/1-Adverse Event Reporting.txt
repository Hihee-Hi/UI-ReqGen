The iTrust electronic healthcare system allows patients to report adverse events related to prescription drugs or immunizations. Patients can view a list of medications or immunizations they have taken or received in the past 12 months, select the item for reporting, describe the adverse event symptoms, and save the information. A fake email is then sent to the prescribing or administering healthcare provider with the patient's details and symptoms. Public health agents can view and manage reports of adverse events related to prescription drugs and immunizations, sorted by NDC or CPT code. They can review the details of each report, contact the reporter for additional information via a fake email, or remove reports if they are found to be invalid or unsubstantiated. If a report is removed, a status change to "removed" occurs, and both the reporter and the involved healthcare provider are notified.
