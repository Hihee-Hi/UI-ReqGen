A patient can view a list of their diagnoses and select one to find LHCPs in their area who have handled that condition in the last three years. The LHCPs are ranked by the number of unique patients treated for the diagnosis. For each LHCP, the patient sees their name, contact information, number of patients treated, prescriptions, lab procedures, and satisfaction ratings. The list is sorted by the date of the last office visit, with an option to toggle whether the LHCP is a designated or undesignated DLHCP. The patient can search for LHCPs by last name, specialty, and zip code.
