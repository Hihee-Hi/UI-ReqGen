The administrator is responsible for managing key healthcare data in the system, including:

1. Immunizations:Maintaining a list of allowable immunizations, with associated CPT codes and up to 30 characters for the immunization name.
2. Diagnoses:Storing allowable diagnoses, including ICD-9CM codes, classification (chronic/short-term), and a 30-character diagnosis name.
3. Drugs:Maintaining a list of allowable drugs, identified by National Drug Codes (NDC).
4. Physical Services:Managing allowable physical services and lab procedures, with LOINC codes.
5. Hospitals:Storing hospital ID numbers and names. The administrator can add or modify hospital names but cannot delete or modify hospital ID numbers.
6. Appointment Types:Adding or modifying appointment types, including name and duration (in minutes), with a unique type name required.
7. Personnel:Adding or editing personnel data for LHCPs, ERs, or public health agents, including name, email, and specialty. LHCPs are assigned to multiple hospitals, and LTs are assigned to a single hospital. The data for personnel is edited according to specified formats.

Additionally, administrators can manage user creation, such as adding LHCPs with specified specialties, assigning them to hospitals, and managing their access credentials.
