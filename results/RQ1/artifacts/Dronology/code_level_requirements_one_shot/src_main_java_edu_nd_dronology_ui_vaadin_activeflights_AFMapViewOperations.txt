In the active flight overview, users have the ability to manage how they view the drones on the map. Users can choose to focus on specific drones they have selected, allowing them to follow their movements closely. Alternatively, they can opt for a broader view that displays all drones collectively on the map, providing them with an overall perspective of the drone operations. This functionality enables efficient monitoring and tracking based on user preferences, ensuring that users can adapt their view according to specific observation needs during drone operations.
