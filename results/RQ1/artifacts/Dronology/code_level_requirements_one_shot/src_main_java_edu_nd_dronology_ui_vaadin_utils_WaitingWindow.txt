When awaiting a process or event to complete, you receive a notification window displaying a message to indicate that you need to wait. This notification window remains on your screen and prevents interaction with other elements until the specified condition is met. Once the condition, signifying the completion of the awaited process, is met, the window closes, allowing you to continue with your tasks. Throughout this duration, an indicator shows that your action is in progress, keeping you informed about the status of the operation.
