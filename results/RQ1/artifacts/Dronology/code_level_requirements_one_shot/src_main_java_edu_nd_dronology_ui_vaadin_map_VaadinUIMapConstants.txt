The user, when utilizing the mapping services, has the capability to switch between multiple map views. They have options such as viewing satellite imagery of the world, a visually light base-map for ease of examination, or focusing on specific maps of the South Bend area which can be accessed both in satellite view or standard view. This flexibility allows for tailored map interaction according to the user's specific geographic and detail-oriented needs, ensuring that they can access the appropriate spatial data in the context of their operational requirements.
