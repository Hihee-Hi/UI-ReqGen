Users are provided with a comprehensive overview of each drone's status through a user-friendly display format. They can view vital information such as the drone's battery level, speed, current location coordinates including latitude and longitude, and altitude. The system color-codes the drone's health condition, allowing users to quickly assess its operational status. Additionally, the name of the drone and its current status are clearly presented, ensuring that users can effectively monitor and manage their fleet of drones in real-time.
