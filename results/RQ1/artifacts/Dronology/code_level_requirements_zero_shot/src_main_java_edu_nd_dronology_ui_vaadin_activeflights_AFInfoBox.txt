As a user interacting with this drone management system, you can view and manage the information of active drones through an information box interface. This box allows you to gain insights into a drone's current status, such as its name, flight status, battery life, location coordinates, altitude, and speed. You can also execute various commands on the drone from this interface. For instance, you can send a command to take off to a specified altitude if the drone is currently on the ground. If circumstances require, you can direct the drone to return immediately to its home base, canceling all other commands. Additionally, you can assign a new flight route or resend the last command if needed. The visibility of certain parts of the information box can be toggled to show more or less detail, depending on what you need at the moment. The interface provides feedback on actions taken, such as notifications when you attempt to take off a drone that is already airborne. The system semantically utilizes icons and descriptions to provide a quick overview of the drone's health and operational status, allowing you to swiftly assess and act according to the drone's current state.
