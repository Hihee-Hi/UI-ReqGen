In this interface, you will have a set of options designed to enhance your aerial monitoring capabilities. You can choose to follow specific aerial vehicles, tailoring your view to focus only on those that are currently active or of most interest to you. Additionally, you have the option to view all vehicles, providing you with a comprehensive overview of all ongoing operations at a glance. This flexibility in viewing options allows you to prioritize and manage your focus effectively, ensuring efficient monitoring and control of your aerial operations. Through intuitive button selections, you can quickly and effortlessly switch between these viewing modes, aligning with your monitoring needs.
