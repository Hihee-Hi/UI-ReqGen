The system allows users to interact with maps that depict flight routes and waypoints. When you move your mouse over a waypoint on the map, a popup will appear showing detailed information about that particular waypoint, such as its latitude, longitude, altitude, and transit speed. This popup can also include a delete button if the map is in an editable state, allowing you to remove the waypoint from the map. The popup's position adjusts dynamically based on your screen's dimensions and resolution to ensure it is prominently displayed regardless of where the waypoint is on the map. Through this interface, users can efficiently navigate and manage flight route waypoints, personalizing the map view according to their needs.
