As a user, you will be able to manage flight routes through a comprehensive interface. Initially, you can view the list of available flight routes. When a specific route catches your attention, clicking on it highlights that route while resetting the colors of other outlined routes for clear distinction. This highlighted route is then visually represented on a map, allowing you to see its exact path. The system also offers clear prompts when you attempt actions that could discard your unsaved work or when you initiate the removal of a flight route, ensuring you're making informed choices. If a process requires additional waiting time, such as retrieving or updating information, you will be conveniently informed to wait, providing a smooth user experience.
