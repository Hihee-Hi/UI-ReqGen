Users of the system benefit from visual cues and icons that enhance their ability to manage and control active flights. Each icon serves a distinct purpose; for instance, different states of drones, such as being in focus, selected, or at a waypoint, are represented by specific images. During interactions in the flight management panel, users can quickly identify the important states of drones using these icons, aiding in intuitive and efficient monitoring of multiple drones simultaneously. Such visual aids are crucial in providing clear, immediate feedback on the operational status of drone fleets.
