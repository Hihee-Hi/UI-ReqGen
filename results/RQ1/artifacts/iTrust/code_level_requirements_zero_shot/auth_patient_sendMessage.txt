This system allows patients to send messages within a healthcare context. As a patient using the system, you can send messages to healthcare providers you have previously designated. You can also send messages on behalf of other patients if they have declared you as their representative. To use this feature, you will first select a healthcare provider or a patient you represent. Once selected, you will enter the subject and body of the message and then send it. If you have no designated healthcare providers or no other patients have declared you as a representative, you will be informed accordingly. This process ensures secure and directed communication between patients and their healthcare team within the clinic's network.
