As a user accessing the appointment management system, you will log in to view your list of scheduled appointments. The interface will display these appointments in a table format, showing key details such as the patient name, type of appointment, scheduled date and time, and the duration of each appointment. If there are any overlapping appointments that may cause scheduling conflicts, these will be highlighted to draw your attention. Additionally, you have the option to view comments or notes attached to each appointment. If there are currently no scheduled appointments, the page will inform you of this. This system is designed to help you efficiently manage and keep track of your appointments, ensuring that both your time and your clients' time are well-spent and organized.
