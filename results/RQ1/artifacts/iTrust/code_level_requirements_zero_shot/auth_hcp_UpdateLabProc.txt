This service enables healthcare providers to update lab procedures for patients. As a user, you can enter the specific laboratory procedures interface using the patient's lab procedure ID. Once there, you will see the current procedure details, including patient information, lab code, and status. You can change the status of the lab procedure, add commentary, and input results as needed. After making the desired updates, click submit to save changes successfully. You will receive a confirmation message if the updates are made successfully. If issues arise, such as incomplete or incorrect details, you will receive specific error messages. Upon completion, you can easily navigate back to the general view of laboratory procedures.
