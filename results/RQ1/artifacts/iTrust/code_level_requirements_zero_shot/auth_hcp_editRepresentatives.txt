To use the system for managing patient representatives, follow these steps: 

1. Ensure you have a patient ID. If you access the page without a patient ID, you will be redirected to the proper page to input or retrieve it.

2. On the page, you can see a list of current representatives assigned to the patient. If no representatives are yet assigned, you will be informed that no representatives are specified.

3. To remove an existing representative, click on the 'Remove' link next to their name. This action will update the list to reflect the changes.

4. To add a new representative, you will use the 'Find User' button next to 'Patient' to search for and select a user to assign as a representative. Once selected, you will see their information in the specified area.

5. After choosing a new representative, submit the form using the 'Represent this patient' button to update the representative list for the patient.

6. If there are any errors in the process, such as entering invalid information or processing issues, you will receive a message indicating the specific error. Otherwise, confirmation messages for successful additions or removals will be displayed.

This system ensures secure and efficient management of representatives for patient records, maintaining a robust method for healthcare professionals to assign or reassign patient accountability effectively.
