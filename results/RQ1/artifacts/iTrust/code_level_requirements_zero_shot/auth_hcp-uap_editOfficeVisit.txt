To edit an office visit record, access the relevant section where you can view and update various details related to a patient's visit. You can change the visit date, hospital, and add notes. Prescriptions can be reviewed or modified; you may add new medications, ensuring there's no conflict with existing prescriptions or allergies. Laboratory procedures associated with the visit can also be updated. Add or remove diagnoses, procedures, and immunizations as needed. If any changes are detected, you'll be notified to confirm actions before finalizing them. In case of contraindications with medications, a warning will prompt an email notification for follow-up. Once satisfied with the updates, changes can be saved, and you'll be redirected or you can choose to cancel the update process.
