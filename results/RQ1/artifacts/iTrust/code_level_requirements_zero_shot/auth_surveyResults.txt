As a user, you can utilize this system to search for healthcare professionals and view survey results based on either their zip code or the hospital they are affiliated with. Begin by navigating to the search form where you are required to enter either a zip code or a hospital ID to proceed with a search, but note that you cannot use both criteria simultaneously. You can also specify a specialty optionally. Once you've provided the necessary information, submit the form to view a list of healthcare providers that match your criteria. The results will provide detailed insights, including the name, address, specialty, and hospital of the healthcare provider. Additionally, you'll see statistical information regarding patient visit experiences, such as average waiting and exam room times, satisfaction with visits and treatments, and the percentage of visits with available satisfaction data. This system enhances your ability to make informed choices by offering a comprehensive overview of healthcare service efficiencies and satisfaction levels at your chosen facility or location.
