As a patient using this system, you can view your message inbox where you have received communications from healthcare providers. Upon accessing your inbox, you can select a specific message to view its details. This includes seeing who the message is from, the subject, and when it was sent. Additionally, you can read the full message content and have the option to reply directly to it. The interface is designed to ensure you see useful information at a glance and respond to communications effectively, ensuring seamless interaction with your healthcare providers.
