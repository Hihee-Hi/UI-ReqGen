As a patient using this system, you can view details of your laboratory procedures by accessing your account and navigating to the relevant page. Once on the page, the system will automatically retrieve and display a list of your lab procedures. Each procedure includes information such as the lab code, status, commentary, results, associated office visit, and the date of the last update. If you have a specific lab procedure in mind, you can provide its identifier to directly view its detailed information. If an invalid identifier is provided, you will be redirected to your calendar. This process ensures you stay informed about your lab results and their current statuses, aiding in maintaining an up-to-date understanding of your healthcare records.
