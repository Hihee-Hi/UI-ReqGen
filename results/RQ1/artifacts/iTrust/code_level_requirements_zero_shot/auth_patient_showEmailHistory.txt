As a user interacting with this system, you can view your history of email correspondence through a centralized interface. Using this system, you will be able to see a detailed record of all past emails, which includes the recipients, subject lines, the message content, and the dates these emails were sent. When you access this section of the portal, the system will retrieve and display a list of your previous emails. If no emails are present in the records, the system will inform you that there are no emails on record. You can expect this feature to help you keep track of your communication history, offering a clear and organized view of your past email interactions.
