In this system, users who are patients can access a section within their healthcare portal to manage their personal health-related communications. By logging into their account, patients can navigate to the messaging area to view all messages sent to them by healthcare personnel, such as doctors or administrative staff. The interface displays the name of the sender, the content of the message, and the date it was received. Patients have the option to reply to messages directly from this interface, promoting efficient communication and follow-up. If there are no messages, the system politely informs the patient that they currently have no messages. This feature ensures patients stay informed and engaged in their healthcare journey, enhancing the overall patient care experience.
