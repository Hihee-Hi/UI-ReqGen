As a user accessing this healthcare platform, you can explore various diagnoses recorded in your medical profile. You'll find a section titled "Diagnoses" which lists the medical conditions along with their descriptions. By clicking on any listed diagnosis, you can delve deeper and view healthcare professionals (HCPs) who have experience with that specific condition. The detailed view provides information such as the number of patients treated by these professionals, the list of prescriptions they have made, associated lab procedures, and ratings on both office visit and treatment satisfaction. This helps you make informed decisions about your healthcare by identifying professionals with relevant experience and expertise in managing specific health issues, along with feedback from other patients.
