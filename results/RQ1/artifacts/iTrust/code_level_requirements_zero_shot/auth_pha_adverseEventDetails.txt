In this user interface, healthcare professionals can interact with a system specifically designed for monitoring medication-related adverse events. Within this context, users can view detailed reports on adverse events filed by patients. Users have the ability to remove specific adverse event reports if they are deemed no longer necessary or relevant. Additionally, there is an option to communicate with patients to gain further insights into their experiences concerning specific medications. This allows for a collaborative effort to ensure patient safety and improve medication strategies. Error messages are displayed when operations cannot be completed, ensuring users are informed of any issues during their interactions.
