In this system, you are interacting with a healthcare platform designed to enable healthcare professionals to manage and view patients' office visit histories efficiently. As a user of this system, you can access a list of past patients along with their details, such as their names, addresses, and the dates of their last visits. You can navigate through a table called 'Past Patients,' which displays these details in a structured manner.

To view a specific patient's past visit details, you can click on the patient's name, which serves as a link to another page where you can edit the patient's health record. This feature appears to be part of a broader patient management system where maintaining accurate and up-to-date information is crucial.

The system is designed to facilitate streamlined data interaction, empowering healthcare professionals to deliver quality patient care by easy access to comprehensive patient histories. This functionality is crucial for healthcare providers to review patient history before consultations, ensuring informed decision-making and continuity of care.
