As a user of this system, you would be able to view your personal information, presented in a clear and organized manner on the screen. You start by accessing a secure area where you can view your profile details. Your unique profile picture will be displayed, followed by your full name, gender, and the location you have registered, including city and state. Your birth date will be shown, along with your blood type. This interface ensures that all your vital personal information is easily visible and accessible, allowing you to confirm the accuracy of the details kept in the system and making sure your records are up-to-date for health management purposes.
