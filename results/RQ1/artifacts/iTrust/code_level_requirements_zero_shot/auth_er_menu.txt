As a user interacting with this system, you will navigate within a digital platform aimed at managing healthcare-related tasks. You will see a section dedicated to reports, where you can access different types of patient information. Specifically, through this section, you can generate or view an emergency patient report. This feature is likely to be used when quick and accessible patient data is required, particularly in urgent medical situations where immediate access to a patient's health record is critical. This ensures healthcare professionals have the necessary information at their fingertips to make informed decisions in emergency scenarios.
