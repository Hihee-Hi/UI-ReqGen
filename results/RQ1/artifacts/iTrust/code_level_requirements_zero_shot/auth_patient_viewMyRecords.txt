To view your medical records, simply log into the healthcare system and navigate to the section for viewing records. In this area, you have access to your personal medical information, including both current and past health records. You can view details such as office visits, prescriptions, family medical history, allergy information, and lab procedure results. If you represent another patient or have the authority to view their records, you can switch to their profile and see their medical details. If any surveys related to office visits are incomplete, you can complete them directly from this page. Additionally, you can report any adverse events related to immunizations you have received. If you have any family medical history recorded in the system, it will be displayed here, showing conditions that your family members may have had. This comprehensive interface ensures that all of your medical information is accessible and manageable in one convenient location.
