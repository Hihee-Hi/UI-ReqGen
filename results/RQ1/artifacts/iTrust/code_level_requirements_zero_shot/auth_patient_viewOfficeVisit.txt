When you visit the office visit details page, you can see a summary of a specific office visit with your healthcare provider. The page presents you with the date of your visit, the name of the health care provider you saw, and any notes taken during the visit. You’ll find a list of diagnoses, if any were made, complete with their codes and descriptions. Furthermore, if you received any prescriptions, they're outlined here with details such as medication codes, descriptions, the period you should take them, dosages, and any special instructions. If any medical procedures were undertaken during your visit, they’re also listed with their relevant codes and descriptions. This organized information helps you track and understand the healthcare services you've received during the visit.
