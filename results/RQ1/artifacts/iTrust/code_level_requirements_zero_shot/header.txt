As a user of this system, you'll seamlessly navigate through a healthcare platform that prioritizes your role-based access to information. Upon entering the platform, you'll experience a personalized interface that caters to various user roles, including patients, healthcare providers, administrators, and more. Your journey begins with a secure login, after which you are directed to a well-structured portal where your specific role unlocks unique menus and options. As a patient, you might access your medical records and appointments. Healthcare providers will see an interface tailored to manage patient interactions and medical data. Administrators will focus on operational functionalities and user management. If your session is active, your name displays at the top, providing quick access to your profile and logout option. In case of an error during your interaction, a clear message will guide you, ensuring an intuitive and straightforward experience on this healthcare platform.
