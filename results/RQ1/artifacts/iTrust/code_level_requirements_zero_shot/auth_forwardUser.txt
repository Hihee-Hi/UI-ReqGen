As a user, upon authentication, you are forwarded to your specific homepage based on your role in the system. If identified as a patient, you are directed to the patient homepage, where you can manage your health information. As a healthcare provider, you are taken to the healthcare provider interface to access patient data and treatment options. Administrative and emergency roles are channeled to their respective sections, allowing access to system management or emergency services. Unauthorized access leads to an error page or redirects to the system's main entry if your session is invalid.
