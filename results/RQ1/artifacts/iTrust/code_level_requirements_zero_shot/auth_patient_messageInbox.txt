If you are a patient using this system, you can manage and view your messages in your inbox. You can access a list of all the messages sent to you, sort them by sender name or the time received, and filter them based on specific criteria like sender, subject, presence or absence of particular words, or date range. You can edit these filter settings and test them to see their effectiveness. Once the setup is saved, you can apply these filters directly to focus on the messages that meet your criteria. To read a message, simply select the 'Read' option next to the message details. Unread messages will appear in bold to help you easily identify them. If you have no messages, the system will inform you of that with a simple message.
