As a user, if you experience an adverse event with medication, you can report it using this system. First, ensure you are logged into your account. Once you're on the reporting page, provide detailed information about the adverse event you've encountered. You will need to enter your comments or description of the incident using the form provided. If you are connected to healthcare professionals, the system allows you to link the report to the prescriber by selecting from a list of associated healthcare providers. After you submit the report, if you have selected a prescriber or your session information contains prescriber details, the system will process your report and notify the necessary medical personnel about the event through emails. If the submission is successful, you will be redirected to the homepage, confirming the report has been logged. Any error during this process will be displayed, allowing you to address issues and resubmit. This system is especially beneficial for patients under the care of healthcare providers who need to ensure any adverse effects of treatments are promptly addressed and communicated in a structured clinical network.
