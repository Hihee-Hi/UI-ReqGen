As a user of this system, you can adjust the duration of time before your session automatically ends due to inactivity. To change the session timeout settings, you will see a prompt to enter the desired number of minutes you would like your account to remain active before requiring re-authentication. Ensure that you input a minimum of one minute. Once you submit your desired timeout duration, the change will be noted, although it will only take effect the next time you log in. A confirmation message will indicate that the session timeout has been updated.
