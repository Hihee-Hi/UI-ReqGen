As a user within a healthcare administrative system, you have the capability to add new Emergency Responders to the system. To do this, you will need to enter the necessary details such as the first name, last name, and a valid email address of the Emergency Responder in question. In cases where an email address is unavailable, the hospital's predefined email address should be used for password recovery purposes. Once the form is completed, the system will process the information and generate a new unique ID and a temporary password for the Emergency Responder. You will be informed of these details on the confirmation screen, where you are encouraged to relay this information to the new Emergency Responder to facilitate their access to the system. This process streamlines the onboarding of Emergency Responders to ensure that they are duly registered and have secure access credentials to perform their duties.
