As a patient visiting the healthcare system, after your appointment with the doctor, you will participate in a feedback survey to share your experience. On the survey page, you're asked to provide details about your visit, specifically how long you waited both in the waiting room and in the examination room before seeing the doctor. You can enter these wait times in the respective fields provided. Further, you will rate your satisfaction with your overall office visit and the treatment or information you received by selecting an option from very satisfied to very unhappy. After filling out the survey, which might include a few different questions for various aspects of your visit, you will submit your responses by clicking the "Submit Survey" button. Once submitted successfully, you will be redirected to a confirmation page indicating your feedback has been recorded.
