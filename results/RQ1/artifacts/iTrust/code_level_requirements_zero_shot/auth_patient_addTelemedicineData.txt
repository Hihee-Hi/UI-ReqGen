As a user, you will navigate to a system that allows you to report medical data for telemedicine purposes. You can either self-report your health data or do so on behalf of another person if you are their representative. Upon entering the page, you may need to specify who the report is for unless you are updating your own data. You are then prompted to fill out a form that collects critical healthcare information, specifically systolic and diastolic blood pressure and glucose levels. After filling out the form, you submit your data for review and addition to the system. Any reporting errors or validation issues will provide immediate feedback to guide you in correcting them. If you are a representative, the system facilitates access to a list of individuals you represent, allowing you to report data on their behalf efficiently.
