To document an office visit, the healthcare professional must first have a patient ID. If no patient ID is present, the system will prompt the user to select one. Once the patient ID is ready, the professional can proceed to either document a new office visit or edit a previous one. The process of documenting a new visit involves a confirmation step where the professional clicks a button to confirm the action, affirming the decision to document the visit for the specific patient. Alternatively, the healthcare professional can choose to review past office visit records for the patient by selecting from a list of previous visits, allowing them to modify or update information as needed. The interface guides the user through these actions with clear prompts and confirmations, ensuring careful documentation and modification of patient visits within a healthcare record system.
