As a user within the healthcare system, you can view detailed information about your prescribed medications. When you access the prescription details page, the system retrieves and displays crucial information about the specific prescription. This includes the doctor's name who prescribed the medication, the medication's name and its dosage, as well as the start and end dates for the prescription. Additionally, any special instructions related to the medication are also provided. If the prescription cannot be found, an error message is displayed. Once you have reviewed your prescription information, you can navigate back to the home page using an option provided on the same page. This functionality ensures that you have clear and detailed access to your medication information, which is vital for managing your healthcare.
