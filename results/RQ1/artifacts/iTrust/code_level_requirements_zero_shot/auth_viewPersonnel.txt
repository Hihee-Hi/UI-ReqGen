This system is an online platform used to manage and view personnel information, specifically designed to display the details of individuals working in a particular organization or facility. When using the system, a user can view comprehensive information about a specific personnel member. This includes full name, specialty, contact information like phone and email, and their office location. A user accesses this information by selecting a personnel member from a list. The system retrieves and displays the information along with a picture of the individual when available. It helps in ensuring that the people associated with the organization can easily access and manage contact and specialty information of colleagues or associates, facilitating better communication and interaction within the organization.
