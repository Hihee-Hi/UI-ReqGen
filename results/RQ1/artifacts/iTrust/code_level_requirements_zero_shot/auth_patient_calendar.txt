As a user accessing the system, you are provided with a personalized calendar view tailored for managing your healthcare appointments. The calendar displays your scheduled medical appointments, office visits, and lab procedures. You can effortlessly browse through different months using navigation links. Each calendar day encapsulates detailed information about your medical engagements. Appointments, office visits, and lab procedures are individually listed with easy-to-access links to view more details about each. You can also identify any conflicts in your appointment schedule directly from this view, allowing you to better manage and coordinate your healthcare commitments efficiently.
