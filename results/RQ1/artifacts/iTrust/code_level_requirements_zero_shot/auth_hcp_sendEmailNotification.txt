As a user, the interface provides you the ability to easily send email notifications if you are a healthcare professional logged into the system. Once logged in, if you choose to send an email, you are directed to a straightforward form where you can draft and send an email to a chosen patient. First, review the recipient’s default email contents that include your details as the sender. You can then fill in or adjust the recipient's email, input a custom subject, and compose or edit the email body text. After completing the form, you can either send the email or reset the fields if needed. Upon sending, the system confirms successful delivery by presenting the sent email details, reassured by greyed-out recalled information. You are also given the option to return to the main home page. This process facilitates efficient communication between healthcare providers and patients, streamlined for quick and effective outreach.
