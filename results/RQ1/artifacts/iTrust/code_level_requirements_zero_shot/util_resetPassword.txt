To reset your password, start by accessing the reset password page which serves a variety of user roles including patients, healthcare professionals, administrative staff, and others. On reaching the page, you will be required to provide your unique user identification number and select your role from a list of available options. If successfully verified as a user within the system, you will then proceed to answer a security question that you previously set up. Once you provide the correct answer, you can enter a new password and confirm it. Ensure your passwords match to finalize the password reset process. If any errors occur during this process, you will be prompted to try again or correct any invalid details provided. The system will prevent too many failed attempts from the same location to ensure security.
