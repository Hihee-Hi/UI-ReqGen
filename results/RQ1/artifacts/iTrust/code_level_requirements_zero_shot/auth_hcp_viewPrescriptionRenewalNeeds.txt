As a user interacting with this healthcare management system, you can view a list of your patients who may need their prescriptions renewed. Once you log in, the system will display a page titled "My Patients with Potential Prescription-Renewal Needs." If there are no patients who currently require a prescription renewal, you will see a message stating, "No patients have renewal needs." Otherwise, the page will list the patients' names, phone numbers, and email addresses. Each patient's name is clickable, allowing you to send them an email notification directly from the system. This functionality helps you effectively manage and communicate with patients whose prescriptions might need attention, ensuring their continuous access to necessary medication.
