As a healthcare provider using this system, you have the ability to monitor patients' remote health data effectively. After logging in, this feature allows you to view a structured list of patient information including their blood pressure readings, glucose levels, and the date of the health data report. Patients' health readings that fall outside of normal ranges are highlighted to capture your attention, helping you quickly identify potential concerns. You can explore additional patient details by selecting individual patient entries. This system ensures that you are well-equipped to provide timely and informed care to your patients by keeping patient data organized and accessible.
