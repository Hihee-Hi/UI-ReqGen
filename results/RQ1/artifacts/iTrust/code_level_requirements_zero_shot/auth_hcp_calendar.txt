As a user of this appointment calendar system, you can view and manage appointments efficiently. Upon accessing the calendar interface, you will first see a display of the current month's appointments in a calendar format. You can navigate between months using "Last" and "Next" links provided, which allow you to explore past and future appointments. Each day on the calendar checks for any appointments scheduled and displays them accordingly. You will notice clear indicators for any dates with appointments and, if applicable, conflicts are highlighted. For further details, you can click on any appointment to read more specific information about it. Overall, this system aids in scheduling by providing a streamlined calendar view to track and explore your appointments over time.
