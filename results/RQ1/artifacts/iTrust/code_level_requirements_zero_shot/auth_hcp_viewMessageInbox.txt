When you access your message inbox, the application presents a list of messages that you have received. If you choose to view a specific message, the system retrieves it from your saved messages and displays its details, such as the sender's name, the subject, the date, and the time it was sent. The complete content of the message is also shown to you. Additionally, you are provided with the option to reply to the message directly from this view, enhancing your ability to communicate efficiently. This system ensures that all your messages are easily accessible, and any unread messages are automatically marked as read once opened. If you attempt to view a message that does not exist or there is an error in accessing your messages, the application gracefully redirects you back to your inbox or a dedicated error page to maintain a smooth user experience.
