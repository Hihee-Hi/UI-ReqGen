As a user engaging with this system, you can gain access by attempting to log in. If the system is undergoing maintenance or cannot connect to its primary environment, it will inform you by restricting access. If your credentials are already recognized, it retrieves your user identity and verifies your permissions. Upon entering your login details, the system checks the validity of your credentials. Should your credentials be valid, you will be successfully logged in and your session will be initiated. However, if they are found to be invalid, your session will be terminated immediately to maintain security, preventing further interaction until valid access is cleared. If your user role changes unexpectedly, the system may redirect you to a specific notification page, indicating a system refresh or update.
