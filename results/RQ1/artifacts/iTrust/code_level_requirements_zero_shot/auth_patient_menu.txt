As a user navigating the healthcare management platform, you have access to a diverse range of functionalities designed to streamline and enhance your experience in managing your health records and interactions with healthcare providers. You can edit your personal details such as address and contact information through the 'Edit My Demographics' section. To stay informed on who accesses your records, you can view a detailed log of access events. By exploring 'Full Calendar' and 'View My Appointments', you can efficiently manage your appointments, ensuring you never miss an important date. The system also offers comprehensive insights into your health conditions by viewing past diagnoses and consulting with your healthcare providers directly. Prescription details are available, including current and expired medications for easy management. Communication is key, and the messaging feature offers you a secure inbox and outbox to stay in touch with healthcare professionals. If remote healthcare is part of your routine, the telemedicine section allows you to report health status updates. Furthermore, the system encourages feedback through satisfaction surveys, and you can review past communications via email history for your records. Overall, this structured menu facilitates a seamless interaction with your healthcare information and services, aligning with modern healthcare demands.
