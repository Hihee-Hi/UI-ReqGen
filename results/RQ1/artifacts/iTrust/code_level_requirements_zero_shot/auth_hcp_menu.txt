If you are using this system, you can manage various healthcare-related tasks through a menu with categorized options. In the 'Add' section, you can add new patients or administrative personnel. The 'Edit' category allows you to update basic health information, personal demographics, patient details, and patient representatives. You can also edit details of administrative personnel. Appointments can be managed by scheduling new ones or viewing existing ones under the 'Appointments' section. The 'Messaging' section lets you access your message inbox and outbox for communications. In the 'View' section, you can look at patient visit history, appointment calendars, lab procedures, and any report requests. You can generate emergency patient reports in the 'Report' category. 'Telemedicine' functionalities include managing patient lists and monitoring patients remotely. Lastly, other tasks such as consultations, evaluating chronic disease risks, documenting office visits, viewing email history, setting visit reminders, analyzing survey results, and assessing prescription renewal needs are available in the 'Other' category. This structure supports efficient management and operational oversight in a healthcare environment.
