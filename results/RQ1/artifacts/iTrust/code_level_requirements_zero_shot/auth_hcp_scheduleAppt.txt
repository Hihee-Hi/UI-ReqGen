To schedule an appointment, start by navigating to the appointment scheduling page. If prompted, verify the patient you are managing or select a different one through the provided link. Once confirmed, proceed to fill out the required information on the form. Select the type of appointment by choosing from the available options which detail duration. Pick a date by clicking the date selection button, followed by setting the appointment time using the dropdown menus for hours, minutes, and AM/PM distinction. You can optionally add comments about the appointment for additional context. After ensuring all details are correct, submit the form to finalize the scheduling of the appointment. Upon submission, if successful, you'll see a confirmation and be redirected to the homepage, optimizing the coordination and preparation for upcoming interactions.
