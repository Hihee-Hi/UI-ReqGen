This system displays an overview of operations conducted within a healthcare context, specifically for users who are involved in testing. When using this system, you will be able to view an Operational Profile that summarizes different types of transactions. This profile is presented in a table format that breaks down the operation types, counts of transactions, and calculates the percentage relative to total transactions, specifically categorized by patients and personnel. The platform elegantly distinguishes between general totals and those attributable solely to patients and personnel, ensuring a comprehensive understanding of operational distribution and engagement. If there is any disruption or issue while you are interacting with the system, you will receive a notification indicating an exception occurred, along with additional information about the error.
