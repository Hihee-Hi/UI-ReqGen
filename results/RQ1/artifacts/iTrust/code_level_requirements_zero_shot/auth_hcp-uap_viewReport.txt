Through this system, a healthcare professional can obtain comprehensive reports on individual patients. By selecting a specific patient, a complete profile is generated, detailing personal information, insurance coverage, health records including metrics like cholesterol levels and blood pressure, history of office visits, diagnoses, documented procedures, prescribed medications, known allergies, and family relations. The report also lists healthcare providers responsible for care and any patients the selected individual represents or is represented by. This enables a streamlined view of the patient's medical history, facilitating informed decision-making and comprehensive patient care review.
