As a user of this system, you access the Reminders Message Outbox to review messages that need your attention. These messages are sorted and displayed based on criteria that you can set. You have the ability to sort messages by the name of the recipient or by the date they were sent. Additionally, you can choose to view these sorted lists either in ascending or descending order. Each message is displayed with information like the recipient, subject, and the date it was sent. Unread messages are highlighted to easily catch your attention. If a message piques your interest, you can click on it to read its details. If there are no messages at the moment, the system will inform you accordingly. This interaction ensures efficient management and tracking of your outgoing reminders, essential for maintaining timely communication and effective message handling.
