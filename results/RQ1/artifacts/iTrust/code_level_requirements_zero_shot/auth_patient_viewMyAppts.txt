As a patient using this system, you can view your upcoming appointments, which are organized in a table format for easy reference. Each appointment entry will display key information, including the patient's name, the type of appointment, the scheduled date and time, and the duration. Should there be any overlap or scheduling conflicts with your appointments, the system will highlight these in bold to draw attention. 

You can check for comments associated with each appointment. If comments exist, you will be provided with a link to read them. For appointments without comments, it'll simply indicate "No Comment." If you don't have any appointments scheduled, the system will clearly inform you by stating, "You have no Appointments." This interaction helps you manage and keep track of your appointment schedule efficiently.
