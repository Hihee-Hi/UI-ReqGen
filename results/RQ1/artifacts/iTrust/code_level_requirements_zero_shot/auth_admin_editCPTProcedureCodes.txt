This system allows healthcare administrators to manage a list of Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) codes used in medical billing and records. As a user, you can view existing procedure codes and their descriptions, identify whether they are related to immunizations, and update or add new procedure codes through a form. The interactions include inputting or selecting a procedure code and description, marking if it's for immunization by checking a box, and submitting the form to add or update the details. If you encounter any errors with the entered data, informative messages will guide you to correct them. This promotes accurate and updated maintenance of medical procedures within the system, critical for efficient healthcare administration.
