This system allows patients to view detailed records of who accessed their medical information, displaying data such as the date of access, the accessor's name, their role, and a short description of the access transaction. Patients can filter the records by specifying a start and end date to view particular entries, and also sort these records by date or the role of the accessor for convenience. If no records are found, the system communicates this to the user, ensuring transparency and ease of use in tracking historical access to their health information.
