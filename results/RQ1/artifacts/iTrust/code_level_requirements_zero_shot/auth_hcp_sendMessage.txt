This system is designed to facilitate communication within a healthcare context by allowing healthcare professionals to send secure messages to their patients. As a user, if you are a healthcare provider logged into the system, you can send a message to a patient by either first selecting a patient or being redirected to a patient selection interface if no patient is currently selected. Once a patient is selected, you can compose a message by entering a subject and the message text in the provided form and then clicking the "Send" button to dispatch the message. If any errors occur during this process, such as a validation error, a message will inform you of the problem, allowing you to address it before resending. Upon successful sending, you will be redirected to view your sent messages. This structured communication process helps maintain confidentiality and organization within healthcare messaging workflows.
