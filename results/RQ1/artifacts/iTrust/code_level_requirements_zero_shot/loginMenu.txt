As a healthcare system user, when you access the login interface, you will be prompted to enter your unique identification number and password. If there has been a previous issue with logging in, you will see a message explaining the problem. For those who might have forgotten their password, an option is available to reset it. This setup is notably part of a healthcare system, which controls access to sensitive medical and personal information by requiring secure login credentials. In a testing environment, mock users with pre-filled credentials can simulate patient, healthcare provider, or administrative roles to ensure the system's functionality. After entering your credentials correctly, you can securely log in to access the system's resources.
