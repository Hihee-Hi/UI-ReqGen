Users accessing this system primarily interact with the functionality to view detailed information about healthcare professionals they have visited. As intended for patients, the system permits selecting specific healthcare providers from a list, displaying essential details such as the provider's name, specialty, contact information, and address. This page is particularly useful for managing patient-provider relationships by allowing patients to review their visits and have ready access to pertinent contact details. Users can easily navigate and identify healthcare providers they have interacted with and their specialties, enhancing their healthcare management experience.
