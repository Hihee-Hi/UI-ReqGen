As a user of this system, you can view and manage laboratory procedures for patients. Initially, ensure you have selected a patient to view their lab results. If you haven't, the system will prompt you to choose a patient. Once you've selected a patient, you will see a table displaying detailed lab procedures, including the patient’s name, lab code, rights, status, commentary, results, office visit ID, and the last updated date. You have several actions available: you can edit the office visit details, manage the privacy settings to allow or disallow viewing of the procedure, and update the procedure itself. The interface will inform you if there are no lab procedures available for the selected patient.
