As a patient using this system, you have the ability to manage your personal and security information conveniently. Through this platform, you can update your demographic details, including your name, contact information, address, and emergency contact details. Additionally, you can maintain your insurance and authentication information. When you make changes to your information, simply submit the form to save the updates. If there are any errors or missing information while submitting, you will receive feedback to correct these before resubmission. Remember, for security purposes, you should use the "Reset Password" feature on the login page if you need to change your password. This system ensures that your personal information is kept up-to-date and secure, offering you peace of mind and efficiency in managing your healthcare records.
