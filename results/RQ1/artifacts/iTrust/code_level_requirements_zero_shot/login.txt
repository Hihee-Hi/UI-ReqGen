To use the medical application, iTrust, users can log in to access various services tailored either for patients or medical staff. As a patient, you will have the ability to maintain and view your medical history and records, communicate with your doctors, choose a primary caregiver, and manage satisfaction questionnaires among other functionalities. For medical staff, iTrust acts as a comprehensive platform to coordinate patient care. This includes sending messages to patients, arranging office visits, diagnosing, prescribing medication, and managing lab results. However, if there are too many unsuccessful login attempts made in a short period, the system temporarily restricts login access to protect user accounts, requesting that you try again after a 15-minute waiting period.
