To use the system that manages patient visit reminders, a healthcare professional accesses a centralized interface designed to streamline patient checkups by identifying individuals who require visits. Upon accessing this system, the professional can select a reminder category from a predefined list, tailored to particular health prompts or requirements. After making a selection and submitting the request, the system produces a list of patients overdue for checkups, highlighting key details such as the patient's name with a direct link for email notifications, contact number, and specific health flags underlining why the visit is necessary. This information assists healthcare providers in efficiently managing patient follow-ups and ensuring adherence to recommended care schedules.
