This system provides staff members with the ability to update their personal information and security settings within a healthcare environment. As a user, if you are a staff member such as a doctor or nurse, you can log in to your account and edit your personal details. This includes updating your name, address, contact information, and security questions for authentication purposes. The form requires you to correctly enter your Personnel ID to access your information. If you fill out the form with all necessary details and submit it, the system will validate your inputs before processing the updates. You will receive a confirmation message if your information is successfully updated. In case of an error, such as incorrect input formats, an error message will guide you on what needs to be corrected. Additionally, if you need to update your password, you are directed to use a provided link on the login page, ensuring the security and integrity of user access credentials.
