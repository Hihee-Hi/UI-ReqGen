As a user engaging with this system, you can manage your sent messages effectively. Begin by navigating to the section labeled "My Sent Messages" where you will see a list of all messages you have sent. If you wish to compose a new message, you can easily do so by selecting the link to compose a message. Furthermore, you have the ability to sort your list of sent messages by recipient name or the time they were sent. This sorting feature allows you to choose whether you want them displayed in ascending or descending order. If there are any messages in your outbox, they will be presented in a structured table showing the recipient, subject, and date sent, with an option to read the full content of any message. If no messages exist in your outbox, you will see a simple note informing you that no messages are present. Through these intuitive features, you can maintain efficient management and review of your sent correspondence.
