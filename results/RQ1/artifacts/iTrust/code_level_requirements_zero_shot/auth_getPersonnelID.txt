To select a personnel member, you will first land on a page with the title "Please Select a Personnel". Here, you have options to search by first and last names of the personnel. By entering the desired names in the corresponding fields and submitting the search form, the system will search for personnel records matching the provided criteria. Once the search is executed, a list of personnel matching your search criteria will be displayed, showing details such as their identification number, first name, and last name. For each personnel member listed, you can click a button associated with their ID to select them. Upon selecting a personnel member, the action redirects to a subsequent workflow or process determined by the system's forward dynamic. This functionality is primarily designed for scenarios requiring user selection, such as roles in healthcare administration where personnel management is essential.
