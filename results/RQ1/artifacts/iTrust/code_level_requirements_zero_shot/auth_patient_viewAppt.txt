As a user interacting with this system, you can view detailed information about your appointments. When you log into your account and proceed to view a specific appointment, you can see details such as the healthcare professional's name, the type of appointment, its scheduled date and time, and the expected duration. If the appointment includes any comments, these will also be displayed. The system ensures that you only access valid appointment records. If you attempt to view an invalid or nonexistent appointment, the system will redirect you to a general appointments page or another appropriate area to prevent potential errors. This system helps in managing and reviewing your healthcare appointments efficiently within a medical or healthcare context.
