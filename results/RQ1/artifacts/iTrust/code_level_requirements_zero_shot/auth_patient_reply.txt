As a user of this medical communication system, you are able to reply to messages sent by your healthcare providers. When you access the reply section, you will see the subject of the original message already prefixed with "RE:" in the subject line, providing continuity in the conversation thread. You can type your response in the provided text area and send it by clicking the send button. The system ensures your reply is sent directly to the sender of the original message. Once you send your reply, you will be navigated back to your message inbox for further management of your communications.
