As a patient using the healthcare message management system, when you access your sent messages, you are able to view detailed information about each specific message in your outbox. First, you navigate to the sent messages section. Once there, you choose a particular message you wish to review from your list of sent messages. The system then displays this message by showing whom it was sent to, the subject line, the date and time it was sent, and the body content of the message itself. Once you have reviewed the message, you can easily return to the list of sent messages to continue managing your sent communications. If there is any issue with accessing a message, such as trying to view a message that doesn't exist, the system will redirect you appropriately to maintain a smooth user experience.
