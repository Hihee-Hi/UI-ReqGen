As a user interacting with this system, you are likely a healthcare professional working within a medical or healthcare management platform. This interface provides a visual representation of adverse events associated with medications or immunizations. These events are captured and compiled into a chart displaying the frequency of reports by month. Upon accessing this feature, you will enter or select a specific code related to medications or immunizations. This will populate the chart with data that has not been flagged for removal, ensuring accuracy and relevance. The display will clearly show the number of adverse events reported over time, aiding in the analysis of trends or spikes in cases. This visualization is designed to support informed decision-making regarding patient safety and medication management by allowing you to easily observe patterns and potentially identify any issues with particular treatments.
