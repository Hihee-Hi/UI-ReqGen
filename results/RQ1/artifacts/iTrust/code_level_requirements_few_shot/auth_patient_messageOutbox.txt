As a patient using this system, you would:
- Log into your account to access your outbox, which contains a list of all the messages you have sent.
- View your sent messages, including details like the recipient's name, the message subject, and the date it was sent.
- Sort these messages by the recipient's name or the date sent, in either ascending or descending order, according to your needs.
- Open individual messages to read the content if needed.
- If you wish to send a new message, you have the option to navigate directly to the message composition page.
This system helps you manage and track communications with your healthcare provider by allowing easy access to your sent messages.
