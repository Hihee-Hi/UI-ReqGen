As a healthcare provider accessing this system, you have a structured menu that allows you to efficiently manage various aspects of patient care and your administrative responsibilities. Within the menu, you can add a new patient to the system, edit Personal Health Record (PHR) or basic health information for existing patients, and even make updates to your personal demographic information. You can also view and manage your report requests, laboratory procedures, and a patient's list associated with telemedicine services. If you need to add telemedicine data, you can report the patient's health status directly from the menu. Moreover, you can document office visits, assess chronic disease risks, review your email communication history, and check satisfaction survey results to ensure comprehensive patient care and engagement. The menu facilitates easy navigation through different functions, ensuring that you can handle patient information and healthcare tasks smoothly and efficiently.
