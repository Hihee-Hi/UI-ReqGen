As a user of this healthcare system, you can review the privacy policy to understand how your Personally Identifiable Health Information (PIHI) is handled. The policy informs you that the system complies with privacy rules set by the Department of Health and Human Services. By using the system, you consent to the collection and storage of your health information for medical treatment and operations. You have the right to revoke this consent in writing. The policy outlines specific scenarios where your information can be used without explicit authorization, such as for treatment, billing, and public health benefits. It assures you of the integrity and security of your information and states that you have the right to access and amend your medical records if needed. If any details are incorrect, you should contact the system administrator or your healthcare provider.
