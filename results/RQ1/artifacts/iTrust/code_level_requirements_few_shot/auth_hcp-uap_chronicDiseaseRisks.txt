As a healthcare provider or user access personnel using this system, you would start by selecting a patient whose chronic disease risk factors you want to review. If you haven't selected a patient yet, the system will redirect you to a page to enter the patient's ID. Once the patient's ID is set, the system will retrieve the patient's chronic disease risk factors. You can view a list of diseases the patient might be at risk for, along with detailed risk factors associated with each disease. This allows you to identify potential health risks and prioritize care for patients based on their specific risk factors. The system ensures accurate assessment of risks, facilitating proactive management of chronic diseases based on tailored health insights.
