As a user interested in Health Care Provider (HCP) survey results, you would: Search for an HCP's survey results by entering either a zip code or a hospital ID on the form. Keep in mind that entering both is not allowed, and you must choose only one. Optionally, you can select a specialty to further refine your search. Upon submitting your query, the system will display a list of HCPs matching your criteria. If no matches occur, a message will inform you to adjust your search. When results are returned, you'll see detailed averages for each HCP related to waiting and exam room times, office visit satisfaction, and treatment satisfaction. You can click on a doctor's name for more details about their services. This process helps you efficiently locate HCP survey results and make informed decisions based on community feedback.
