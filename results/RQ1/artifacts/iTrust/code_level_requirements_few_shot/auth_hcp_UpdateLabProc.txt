As a healthcare provider using this system, you would update lab procedure details for your patients. You would start by selecting the specific lab procedure you wish to update. Once selected, you can modify the procedure's status, provide additional commentary, and enter the results of the lab. After making the necessary updates, you submit the form to save the changes. The system will confirm that the information was updated successfully. This allows you to efficiently manage and document lab procedures while keeping all necessary details up-to-date for future reference.
