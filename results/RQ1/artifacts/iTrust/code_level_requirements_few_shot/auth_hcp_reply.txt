As a healthcare provider, you can quickly respond to a message from a patient using this system. After entering the reply page, you will see the subject pre-filled with "RE:" followed by the original subject, allowing you to maintain clarity in the conversation thread. You can then type your message in the provided text area. Once you're ready to send your response, simply click the “Send” button. The system will direct the message to the patient's inbox while ensuring the conversation continuity by linking your reply to the original message. This streamlined process helps you communicate efficiently with your patients, ensuring timely and organized correspondence.
