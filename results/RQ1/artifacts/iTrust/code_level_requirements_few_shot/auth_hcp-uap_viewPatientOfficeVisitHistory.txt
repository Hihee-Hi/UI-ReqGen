As a healthcare provider, you can view a list of patients who have visited your practice. The system provides a straightforward view with each patient's name, address, and the date of their last visit. You can click on a patient’s name to access their personal health record for updates or further actions. This function helps you maintain comprehensive records of patient interactions and aids in reviewing histories for ongoing or follow-up care. It is tailored to support efficient patient management in outpatient clinics or private practices.
