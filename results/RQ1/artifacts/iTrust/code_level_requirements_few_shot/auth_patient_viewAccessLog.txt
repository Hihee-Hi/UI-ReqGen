As a patient using this system, you would be able to view a log of who has accessed your health information. You would begin by specifying a date range to filter the access records, allowing you to narrow down the time period you are interested in reviewing. Once the date range is set, you can see a list of all personnel or any representatives who have accessed your records within that period. Each entry in the log will show you the date of access, the name of the person who accessed your information, their role, and a description of the access. You have the option to sort the access entries either by date or by the role of the accessor. If you find any unauthorized access, you can take it up with the concerned authorities for further investigation. This tool helps keep track of your data privacy and ensures transparency in who accesses your health information.
