As a patient using this system, you would log in to view your upcoming appointments scheduled with various healthcare providers. You can view details such as the appointment type, date and time, and duration on a single page. If any appointments conflict due to overlapping schedules, they will be visually highlighted, indicating the need for rescheduling. You can also access comments related to each appointment by clicking on the provided link if available. If there are no upcoming appointments, the system will inform you accordingly, ensuring you remain updated with your healthcare schedule.
