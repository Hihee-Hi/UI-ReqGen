As an administrator using this system, you can manage interactions between drugs based on their ND Codes. Firstly, you select two drugs from the respective dropdowns, where each is represented by its ND Code along with its description. After selecting the drugs, you provide a description detailing the nature of the interaction you want to report. Once you submit, the system processes and records this potential interaction in the database, allowing you to maintain an updated record of drug interactions. If there are form errors or validation issues, appropriate error messages will be displayed to help you correct them. You can always navigate back to the main list of ND Codes if needed. This functionality helps in ensuring patient safety by tracking and alerting drug combinations that may lead to adverse reactions.
