As a public health administrator (PHA) using this system, you will be able to:  
Specify a date range to monitor adverse events related to prescriptions or immunizations, which are reported during that period.  
Select either prescriptions or immunizations to generate a report of adverse events within the chosen time frame.  
View details for each reported adverse event, including the drug or immunization code, the reporter's identity, and the date of the event.  
Click on individual events to see more comprehensive information for further investigation.  
Generate visual representations (charts) tracking adverse events over time for specific drugs or immunizations, which allows you to identify trends and ensure timely intervention.  
The system facilitates efficient data tracking and analysis, helping you detect patterns or high-risk areas that require additional attention, which is crucial for maintaining public health safety and compliance.
