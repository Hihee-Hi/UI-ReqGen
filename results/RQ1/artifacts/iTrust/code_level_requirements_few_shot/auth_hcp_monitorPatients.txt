As a healthcare provider using this system, you would access a list of patients under your care with remotely monitored health data. The list provides detailed information for each patient, such as their blood pressure and glucose levels, along with the date when the data was recorded. The system automatically highlights patients whose current day's data is missing in red, and those with abnormal health readings in yellow, allowing you to prioritize those patients. For more detailed information on a patient's condition, you can select their name from the list. This feature enables the efficient monitoring of patients’ health metrics and assists in quickly identifying potential health issues that require your attention.
