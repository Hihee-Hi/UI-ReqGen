As an administrator using this system, you would: Insert the first name, last name, and email address of the new Emergency Responder into the fields provided on the form. Once you submit the form, the system will create a new account for the Emergency Responder and assign them a unique ID known as the Medical ID (MID). The system shall also generate a temporary password for the new Emergency Responder which you will see after a successful submission. It is your responsibility to convey this important information – the MID and temporary password – to the new Emergency Responder. After seeing the confirmation message, there is an option to proceed to manage further personnel information through an available link. The process is designed to ensure the secure addition of new personnel while maintaining the confidentiality of sensitive information like passwords.
