As a healthcare provider (HCP) using this system, you would: Access your outbox to view all the messages you have sent. You can sort these sent messages based on the recipient's name or the time they were sent, and choose the order as ascending or descending. Click the button to apply your sorting preference. View a list of sent messages displaying the recipient, subject, and sent date of each message. For detailed information, click on the "Read" link next to a message to open and read the full content. If you have no sent messages, a notification will inform you that your outbox is empty. There is also a convenient link for composing a new message, facilitating clear communication with patients or colleagues. This system optimizes your communications by allowing you to effortlessly track and manage sent messages for better organizational workflow.
