As a user of this system, once you log in, you will be directed to the home page that corresponds to your role. For example, if you're a patient, you'll be directed to the patient's home page where you can access health information relevant to you. If you're a healthcare provider, you'll be taken to a home page containing clinical tools and patient records needed for your daily tasks. Similarly, other roles such as User Access Personnel, Emergency Responder, Public Health Authority, Administrator, or Tester have their distinct home pages ensuring they have access to functionalities unique to their responsibilities. If your role is not recognized, you may experience restricted access, leading to a page that highlights access issues. Additionally, invalid sessions or non-existent user IDs may result in the termination of your session or a redirection back to the login page to ensure the security of user information. This system ensures each user has swift access to the tools and data needed for their specific functions.
