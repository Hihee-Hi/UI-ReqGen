As a patient using this system, you have access to an appointment calendar where you can view your scheduled appointments, office visits, and lab procedures. You can navigate through months to manage your schedule effectively. The calendar highlights the current day and organizes your healthcare-related activities based on dates. For each day, you can see detailed descriptions of appointments, the type of office visits, and lab procedures, adding convenience to your healthcare management. Clicking on the activity's details provides further information, assisting you in staying informed about your health activities and results. This seamless interaction helps in keeping track of upcoming health-related tasks and efficiently managing your medical requirements.
