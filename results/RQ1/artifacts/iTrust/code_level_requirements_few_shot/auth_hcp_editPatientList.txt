As a healthcare provider using this system, you manage the list of patients who require monitoring. Initially, you select a patient whose monitoring status you wish to adjust. If the patient exists in the current monitoring list, you have the option to remove them; if they are not on the list, you can add them. When you confirm the action, the system updates the patient’s monitoring status and notifies you with a confirmation message indicating whether the patient was added or removed from the list. You also have the flexibility to choose a different patient if needed before finalizing your decision. This functionality helps maintain an accurate list of monitored patients, ensuring effective management of their healthcare needs.
