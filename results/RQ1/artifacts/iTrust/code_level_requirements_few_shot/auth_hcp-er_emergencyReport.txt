As a healthcare provider (ER staff) using this system during an emergency, you would:  
Access a patient's emergency report by initially entering the patient's ID. If the ID is valid, the system retrieves emergency-specific medical information for you to review.  
You have the ability to print the emergency report, ensuring you have a hard copy of the patient’s critical health information.  
The system automatically notifies all declared healthcare providers (HCPs) of the patient, via email, that the patient’s record has been accessed in an emergency context. This maintains a collaborative communication loop among all relevant medical personnel.  
Within the report, you will see the patient’s demographic details, such as name, age, and gender, as well as their emergency contact information.  
The report provides detailed lists of any known allergies, medical diagnoses, current prescriptions, and immunization history, crucial for quick reference and decision-making in an emergency situation.  
Any warnings or alerts related to diagnoses or medications are prioritized to help you quickly assess any critical health issues that need immediate attention.  
This functionality ensures that, as an ER staff member, you are equipped with comprehensive and up-to-date patient information critical to delivering appropriate and immediate care during emergencies.
