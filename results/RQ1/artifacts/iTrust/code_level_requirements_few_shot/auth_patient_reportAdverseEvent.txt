As a patient using this system, you would be able to report any adverse reactions or issues encountered with medications. You start by accessing the adverse event reporting form, where you're prompted to describe the adverse event in the provided text area. Once you have filled out the details, submitting the form will send your report about the medication reaction to the relevant healthcare provider. Depending on your access, the system might link your report to the specific healthcare provider or prescription that you had interacted with. This ensures that the medication prescriber is notified about the adverse event, helping them manage the patient's care effectively. This process enables a seamless communication channel between patients and healthcare providers regarding medication safety and efficacy.
