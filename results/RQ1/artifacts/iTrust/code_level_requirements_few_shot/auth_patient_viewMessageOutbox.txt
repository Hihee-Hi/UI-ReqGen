As a patient using this system, you can view the details of a message you have sent. You will select a message from your outbox and the system will display the recipient, subject, date, and time it was sent, along with the message content. If you encounter any issues with selecting a message, you will be redirected to try again. After reviewing the message, you have the option to navigate back to the outbox to view other messages. This process ensures you can effectively manage and review your sent communications within the healthcare system.
