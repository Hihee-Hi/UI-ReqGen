As a patient or their designated representative using this system, you would:

Be able to report telemedicine data such as blood pressure (systolic and diastolic) and glucose levels. If you are a patient, you can report your own data, or you may report data for someone you represent, such as a family member.

Simply input the relevant physiological data values into the provided fields and submit the information using the form.

If you are a representative, you can select the patient for whom you're reporting from the list displayed, allowing you to manage the health data of multiple individuals efficiently.

Upon submission, the system records and stores the data securely, ensuring accurate tracking for remote monitoring purposes.

Any mistakes or incorrect entries, such as non-numeric values, will trigger error messages, allowing you to correct any issues before resubmitting the information.

Through this process, you can ensure that both your own and represented individuals' health metrics are up to date and available for remote health tracking.
