As a healthcare provider using this system, you would:
Access your scheduled appointments to view details about each appointment including patient names, appointment types, date and time, and duration.
The system checks for any overlapping appointments, which are displayed with bold highlighting to alert you to potential scheduling conflicts.
For appointments with comments or special notes, the system provides a link to view these comments in detail.
If no appointments are scheduled, the system informs you accordingly.
This process enables you to manage and review your appointments efficiently, ensuring that any conflicts are promptly identified and addressed to maintain smooth scheduling and patient care.
