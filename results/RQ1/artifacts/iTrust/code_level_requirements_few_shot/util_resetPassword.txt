As a user in the system, you would:
Access the reset password functionality to regain access to your account in case you have forgotten your password.
Initially, you will be asked to provide your role (such as patient, healthcare provider, etc.) and your Medical ID (MID) to verify your identity within the system.
If your MID and role are correctly identified, the system will present you with your security question, which you need to answer correctly to proceed.
Upon answering the security question correctly, you will be prompted to set a new password and confirm it for consistency to finalize the password reset process.
If the security question has not been set up for your account, the system will inform you that you need to set one before you can reset your password.
If the answer to the security question or the new password details are incorrect, you will receive appropriate error messages and be given the opportunity to attempt the process again.
This ensures that only users with verified identities can reset their passwords, maintaining the security and integrity of user accounts within the system.
