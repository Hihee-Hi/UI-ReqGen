As a healthcare administrator using this system, you would:
Maintain an up-to-date listing of hospitals by adding new hospitals or updating the information of existing ones.
You can enter the hospital's ID and name in the form provided. The hospital list you maintain is then displayed for your review.
To add a new hospital, input the hospital's details and click "Add Hospital." To update information, select a hospital from the list, fill in the desired changes, and click "Update Hospital Information."
If you make any errors during the update, such as invalid information, a validation error message will appear to inform you, allowing corrections.
The current list of hospitals will be displayed with the ability to quickly select and edit hospital information directly within the interface.
This tool helps streamline the management of hospital records, ensuring accuracy and accessibility for hospital administrators.
