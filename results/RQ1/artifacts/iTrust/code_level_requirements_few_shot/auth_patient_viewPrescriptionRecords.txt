As a patient using this system, you would:
View your current prescription records or those of other individuals you represent.
Select to view your own prescriptions by clicking the "View Current" button or choose another person you represent from a dropdown list, if applicable, to view their prescription records.
See a list of prescribed medications, including details like ND Code, description, duration of the prescription, and the healthcare provider who prescribed it.
For your own prescriptions, you have the option to report any adverse events related to your medication, ensuring that potential drug issues can be tracked and addressed.
If you want to report any adverse events, mark the relevant prescriptions, and the system will guide you to do so.
The system helps you manage medication records efficiently and communicate safety concerns effectively.
