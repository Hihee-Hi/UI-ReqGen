As a public health administrator (PHA) using this system, you would:
Access data related to adverse events associated with prescriptions or immunizations. These events may involve various health issues reported by patients.
View a detailed chart that visually represents the number of adverse events reported by month. This helps in analyzing trends and identifying periods with high incident rates.
Each chart is specific to a particular prescription or immunization code, allowing you to focus on individual medications or vaccines.
The system automatically filters out events that have been discarded or marked as irrelevant, ensuring you have accurate data for decision-making.
The graphical representation provides an intuitive overview, which aids in monitoring public health and taking appropriate measures promptly.
This functionality assists you in tracking public health concerns and crafting informed responses to adverse event occurrences.
