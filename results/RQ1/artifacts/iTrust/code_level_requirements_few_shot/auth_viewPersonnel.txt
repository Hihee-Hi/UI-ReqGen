As a user interacting with the system, you would select a specific personnel from a list to view their detailed profile. You would be able to see their full name, specialty, location details (including the complete address), contact number, and email address. A picture associated with their Medical ID (MID) will also be displayed. This functionality is particularly helpful for efficiently managing and accessing personnel information within the healthcare context, allowing easy retrieval of critical contact and professional details necessary for internal coordination and patient referrals.
