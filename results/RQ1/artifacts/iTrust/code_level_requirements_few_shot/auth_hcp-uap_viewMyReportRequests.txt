As a healthcare provider (HCP) or user access personnel (UAP) using this system, you can view, manage, and fulfill report requests for patients under your care. You'll see a list of all past report requests you've made, along with details such as the patient’s name, the date the report was requested, the date it was viewed, and the current status of each request. For any pending request, you can click on the "View" link to see detailed information contained in the report. Additionally, you have the option to initiate a new report request for a patient by clicking on the provided link, which will take you to a page where you can select a patient and submit your request. This functionality supports efficient tracking and management of patient reports, ensuring you have timely access to the information needed for patient care decisions.
