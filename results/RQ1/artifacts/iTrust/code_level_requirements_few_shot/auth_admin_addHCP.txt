As an administrator using this system, you would:
Enter the healthcare provider's (HCP) first name, last name, and email address to register them in the database.
After you submit the form, the system will automatically generate a unique Medical ID (MID) for the new HCP.
The system will also generate a temporary password that you will need to provide to the new HCP for login purposes.
The successful addition of the HCP will be confirmed with a message displaying the HCP's MID and temporary password.
From there, you can continue to manage the new HCP's information or update their details in other sections of the system.
This process allows you to efficiently add new healthcare providers to the system while ensuring their credentials are securely initialized.
