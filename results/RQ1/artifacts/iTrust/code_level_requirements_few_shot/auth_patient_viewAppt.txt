As a patient using this system, you would first select an appointment from a list of your scheduled appointments. Upon choosing an appointment, you will be presented with detailed information about that appointment. This includes the healthcare provider assigned to the appointment, the type of appointment, the scheduled date and time, and the expected duration. If there are any comments associated with the appointment, those will also be displayed. The system ensures that you can easily view all necessary details for your upcoming appointments, helping you manage your healthcare interactions effectively. If you ever encounter an issue, like selecting an appointment that doesn't exist, the system will redirect you to a relevant page to guide you back on track.
