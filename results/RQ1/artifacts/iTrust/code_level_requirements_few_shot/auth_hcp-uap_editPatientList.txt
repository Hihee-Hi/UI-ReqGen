As a healthcare provider using this system, you would:  
First ensure that you have a patient's Medical ID (MID) available as it is required to proceed.  
If the patient ID is not found or provided, the system will redirect you to obtain the patient ID before continuing.  
Once a valid patient ID is recognized, the system will display whether the patient is currently on your monitoring list.  
Based on their current status, you'll see an option to either "Add" or "Remove" the patient from your list.  
Confirming this action will either add the patient to or remove them from your monitoring list, and the system will confirm your action with a message stating the change.  
Additionally, you have the option to opt for a different patient if needed, prompting the system to reset the patient ID and let you select another patient for editing.  
This tool facilitates easy management and monitoring of your patient's list, ensuring that their status is up to date with your professional monitoring activities.
