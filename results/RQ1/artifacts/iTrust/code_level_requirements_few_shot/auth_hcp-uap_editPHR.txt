As a healthcare professional using this system, you can efficiently manage and update a patient's personal health records through a comprehensive interface. Start by accessing the patient's record, where you can view and edit detailed personal information such as contact details and insurance provider information. You can also review the patient's basic health records, including height, weight, smoking status, blood pressure, and cholesterol levels. If there is any new health information, such as allergies or chronic disease risk factors, you can add or update it using the provided forms. The system allows you to examine the patient's family medical history to assess genetic risk factors. Furthermore, you can navigate through the patient's office visit history and even view the details of any chronic disease threats. The system organizes this information to streamline patient care, making it easy for you to maintain accurate and up-to-date medical records.
