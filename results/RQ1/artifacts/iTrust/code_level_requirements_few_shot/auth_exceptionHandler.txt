As a user experiencing an error on the healthcare management system, you would:
Encounter an unexpected issue that interrupts the normal operation, such as a failed data operation or access problem.
The system will automatically capture the error details and record them for diagnostic purposes.
You will be redirected to a specific page designed to guide you to the appropriate section post-error, ensuring minimal disruption to your workflow.
This system enhances reliability by offering a user-friendly approach to manage and recover from errors.
