As a patient using this system, you would: Access your expired prescription records through your account. You will see a list of all prescriptions that have passed their effective duration, with details including the medication code, description, the period the prescription was active, and the name of the healthcare provider who prescribed it. If no expired prescriptions are found, you will be notified that there are no records. You can click on each prescription to view more detailed information regarding that medication if needed. This function allows you to easily keep track of your medication history and provides contact information for healthcare professionals associated with each prescription.
