As a healthcare provider using this system, you would:
Access the patient's records by first selecting the patient ID, ensuring that the correct individual is being updated.
Once verified, you will proceed to edit various personal, contact, health, and insurance details of the patient.
You can update basic information like name, email, address, and phone numbers, as well as more sensitive details like medical data, including blood type, ethnicity, and gender.
Other fields that can be edited include the patient's emergency contact information, insurance details, and health records such as topical notes or historical diagnosis codes.
For deceased patients, you may enter the date of death and cause of death.
This allows you to comprehensively manage a patient's medical and personal records, ensuring all changes are accurately logged.
Once the information is updated, you will receive a confirmation message.
Password changes are handled separately through a password reset function accessible on the system’s login page.
This process provides streamlined, secure management of patient demographics and health-related records, which is crucial for maintaining updated patient information.
