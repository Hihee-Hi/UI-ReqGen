As a healthcare provider using this system, you can send email notifications to patients. First, you will select a patient from your roster whose Medical ID (MID) is available. After selection, the patient's email address and name will be automatically filled into the email form. You can customize the subject and message before sending the email. You need to provide personalized information in the email text to remind or inform the patient. Once you submit the form, the email is sent using the details provided, and you will receive a confirmation indicating that the email was sent successfully. After sending the email, you will have the option to return to your home page to continue with other tasks. This system makes it easier for healthcare providers to communicate important information directly to patients, ensuring streamlined and efficient healthcare service notifications.
