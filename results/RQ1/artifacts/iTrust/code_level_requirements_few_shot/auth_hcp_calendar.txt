As a healthcare provider using this system, you would:

View your appointment calendar, which allows you to manage and keep track of scheduled appointments for the current month.
Navigate between months using the "Last" and "Next" links to view past and upcoming months' appointments.
Each day in the calendar will display appointments scheduled for that date, with a convenient link to "Read Details" for each appointment, allowing you to view more information.
If an appointment has conflicting schedules or issues, it will be visibly highlighted for immediate attention.
This calendar helps you efficiently plan your daily, weekly, and monthly schedules by clearly presenting your engagements and potential conflicts upfront.
