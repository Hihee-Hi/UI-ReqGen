As a patient using this system's survey function, you would complete a feedback form following your office visit. Firstly, you will enter the length of time you spent in the waiting room and examination room during the visit. This helps the healthcare provider assess efficiency in process flow and patient throughput. Next, you are invited to rate your satisfaction with the visit and the treatment or information received using a scale from 1 (very unhappy) to 5 (very satisfied). Once you submit your responses, the survey data will be stored and associated with the specific office visit you had. This enables healthcare providers to evaluate service quality and make improvements based on patient experiences, ultimately enhancing patient care and satisfaction in future visits.
