As a healthcare provider utilizing this system, you would: Select a patient from your list to view detailed assessments of their remote monitoring data, which include blood pressure readings and glucose levels. Within the patient's details, you also gain access to their representatives, and these are presented should the patient require assistance in managing their health information. For periods when the physiological data was recorded, you can specify a date range to retrieve the patient's health statistics such as systolic and diastolic blood pressure, glucose levels, and the name of the reporter who recorded the data. The system provides visual alerts: days with missing data are highlighted in red, while days with abnormal readings are in yellow, helping to prioritize your review of patient records. This functionality aids in maintaining high-quality monitoring of patient health data, enhancing patient care through informed decisions.
