As a patient using this system, you are able to send messages to your healthcare providers (DLHCPs) or send messages on behalf of other patients who have designated you as their representative. To send a message, you first select the intended recipient from the list of your healthcare providers or from the list of healthcare providers of the patient you represent. After selecting your recipient, you compose your message by entering a subject and the message body. Once the message is composed, you submit it to be sent to the recipient. If you have not set up any healthcare providers or have not been designated as a representative by other patients, the system will inform you accordingly. This messaging system facilitates communication between patients and healthcare professionals, ensuring that messages are delivered to the correct individuals efficiently.
