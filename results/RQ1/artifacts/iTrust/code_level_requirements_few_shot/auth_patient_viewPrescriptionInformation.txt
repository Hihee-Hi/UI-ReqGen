As a patient using this system, you have the ability to view detailed information about your prescriptions associated with a particular office visit. Upon accessing the prescription information page, the system retrieves and displays the following information: the prescribing doctor's name, the medication's details including its description and code, dosage, the start and end dates for the medication, and any instructions for taking the medication. If there are issues finding your prescription, an error message will appear indicating the prescription is unavailable at that time. This process allows you to manage and track your medication easily to ensure you follow your healthcare provider's instructions correctly.
