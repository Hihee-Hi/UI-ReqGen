As a healthcare provider using this system, you would:
Select the type of reminder you want to view from a dropdown menu. This may include various categories such as upcoming visits, checkup reminders, or other healthcare prompts.
Upon selecting and submitting your choice, the system will display a list of patients who need visits based on the selected reminder type.
For each patient, you’ll see their name, phone number, and reasons detailing why a visit is necessary.
The patient’s name is linked to an option to send an email notification directly to remind them of their visit needs.
This functionality enables you to efficiently manage patient visit reminders, thereby ensuring patients receive timely care and you maintain effective communication.
