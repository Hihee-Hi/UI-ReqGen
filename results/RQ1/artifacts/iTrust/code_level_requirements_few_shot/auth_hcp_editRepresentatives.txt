As a healthcare provider, you are able to manage patient representation within your system. First, you must ensure that you have selected a valid patient. Once a patient is selected, you can view their current list of representatives. If there are no representatives, this will be clearly indicated. For each representative, you have the option to remove them if they are no longer needed. Additionally, you can add a new representative for the patient by searching for a user, selecting them, and confirming your choice. This system is designed to let you efficiently manage the representatives to ensure that the patient's information is accessed and handled by the appropriate individuals.
