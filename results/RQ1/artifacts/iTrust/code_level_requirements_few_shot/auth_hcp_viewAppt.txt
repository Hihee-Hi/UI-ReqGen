As a healthcare provider using this system, you would:
Utilize the system to view detailed information about your appointment with a patient, which includes the patient's name, the type of appointment scheduled, the date and time of the appointment, and its duration. You will also see any comments related to the appointment. If the appointment details are not accessible initially, the system redirects you to another page listing your appointments or notifies you of an error. This setup ensures that you can verify appointment details and plan accordingly for your patient's visit.
