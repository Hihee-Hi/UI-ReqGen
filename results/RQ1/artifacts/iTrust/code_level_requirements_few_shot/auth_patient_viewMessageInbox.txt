As a patient using this system, you would access your message inbox to view messages sent to you by healthcare professionals or administrative staff. When selecting a specific message, the system retrieves that communication's details, including the sender's name, subject, and the date and time it was sent. You'll be able to read the message in full and have an option to reply directly through the system's interface. This feature ensures efficient communication between you and your healthcare providers, allowing for fast and organized exchanges of information.
