As a user trying to access this medical system, you are provided a login page for authentication. Upon entering the login details, the system checks their validity. If login attempts fail repeatedly in a short period of time, the system will temporarily block access for 15 minutes to prevent unauthorized access, safeguarding sensitive medical data. Successfully logging in grants you access to a comprehensive suite of features within the system, which include managing your medical history, communicating with healthcare providers, scheduling appointments, and viewing lab results, ensuring your healthcare journey is streamlined and efficient. This approach prioritizes security while offering robust features for both patients and medical staff.
