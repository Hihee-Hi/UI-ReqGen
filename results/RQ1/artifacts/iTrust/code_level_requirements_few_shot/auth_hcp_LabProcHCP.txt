As a healthcare provider utilizing this system, you will be able to view and manage laboratory procedures for patients under your care. First, you must access a patient's record by their unique ID. If the ID is missing or incorrect, the system will redirect you to obtain the correct patient identifier. Once authenticated with the patient ID, you can see a list of lab procedures associated with the patient. For each procedure, you can review various details including the lab code, rights granted on the procedure, current status, any commentary or results available, the associated office visit ID, and the date it was last updated. The system allows you to edit the office visit details if you have the appropriate access rights. You also have the ability to change the privacy settings of the lab procedure, allowing you to control who can view it. Additionally, there is an option to update the procedure's information if needed. If no data is available for the procedures, the system will clearly communicate this to you. This setup ensures that you manage your patient's laboratory information effectively while maintaining control over access to sensitive data.
