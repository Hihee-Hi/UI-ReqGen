As an administrator using this system, you would: Enter the new Public Health Administrator's first name, last name, and email address into the form provided. After submitting the form, the system will create a new record for the administrator and generate a unique employee identifier, known as a Medical ID (MID), for them. Along with the MID, a temporary password will also be generated. This information should be communicated to the new administrator so that they can access their account and reset their password. A message confirming the successful addition and the relevant details will be displayed. This process ensures that new Public Health Administrators are added efficiently, with secure access credentials established upon entry into the system.
