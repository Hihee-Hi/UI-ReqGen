As a healthcare provider using this system, you would begin by selecting a specific patient and their associated office visit to access the current prescriptions. Should you need to update a prescription, you can initiate an edit process directly from the displayed list of medications. Upon selecting a prescription, you will be provided with a form where you can adjust details such as the dosage and any special instructions for use. Once you have made the desired changes, you can submit the form to update the prescription while ensuring the medication's start and end dates remain the same. If the update is successful, you will see a confirmation message with the revised prescription details. If the system encounters any issue during the process, an error message will display, allowing you to attempt the update again. This functionality ensures that prescription information remains current and accurate, supporting patient care by providing clear and accessible medication instructions.
