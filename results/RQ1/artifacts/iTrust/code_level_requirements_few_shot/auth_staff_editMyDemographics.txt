As a staff member using this system, you would:  
Access your personal demographic information page, which allows you to update your details securely.  
Fill in your personal information such as first name, last name, address, city, state, zip code, and phone number in the provided form fields.  
Ensure that your authentication information is updated by setting a security question and answer. This is essential for the security and recovery of your account.  
If you need to change your password, you have to use the "Reset Password" option available on the login page, as this page focuses on demographic data update only.  
Submit the form to update your details, and you will see a confirmation message indicating successful information update.  
The system uses these updates to maintain accurate records of the personnel, ensuring efficient communication and security within the healthcare organization.
