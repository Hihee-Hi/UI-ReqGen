As a patient using this system, you have access to various self-service functionalities conveniently organized under different categories. You can edit personal details such as demographics to ensure your information is up-to-date. The system enables you to view your access log, check your calendar for important dates, review past diagnoses, and see a list of healthcare providers you have visited. To keep track of your medical history, you can view your medical records and prescription details, including any expired prescriptions.

Consult the appointments section to manage your schedule and view upcoming visits to your healthcare providers. For effective communication, use the messaging section to check your inbox and outbox for correspondence with healthcare professionals. If you are enrolled in remote monitoring, report your current health status under telemedicine to keep your care providers informed.

Finally, for additional insights, you may view satisfaction survey results and examine your email history for any communication with the system or your healthcare team. This structured menu empowers you to manage your health-related tasks and communications with ease and transparency.
