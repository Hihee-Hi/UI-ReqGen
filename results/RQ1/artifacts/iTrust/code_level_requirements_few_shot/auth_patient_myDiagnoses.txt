As a patient using this medical system, you can access your list of diagnoses. The system presents each diagnosis with a description, which can be clicked to view more information about healthcare professionals (HCPs) who have experience with that specific diagnosis. If you select a diagnosis, you will see a list of HCPs, detailing the number of patients treated for the diagnosis, any prescriptions they have ordered, lab procedures conducted, and satisfaction ratings for office visits and treatments. This helps you to understand your diagnosis better and provides insight into the professionals skilled in managing your condition, potentially assisting you in choosing healthcare providers based on their experience and patient satisfaction.
