As a patient using this system, you have the ability to reply to messages you receive from healthcare providers. When you access the reply page, ensure you have a previous message selected to reply to. The system will retrieve the sender's information and the subject line of the original message, preparing a response draft with a prefixed subject indicating it's a reply. Fill in a new or continued subject if necessary and compose your reply in the textbox provided. Once you have reviewed your message, click the "Send" button to forward the message back to the original sender. If the required elements are missing, the system redirects you to your inbox, guiding you to initiate your reply from there, ensuring efficient communication with your healthcare provider.
