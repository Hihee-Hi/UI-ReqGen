As a healthcare provider using this system, you can easily communicate with your patients through secure messaging. If you want to send a message, you first choose the patient you wish to contact. If you haven't selected a patient, the system will prompt you to do so. Once a patient is selected, you can compose your message by entering a subject and the message content. After writing your message, you can send it directly to the patient. Should you enter any invalid information, the system will alert you so you can correct it before sending. This tool streamlines communication, allowing you to stay in touch with patients effectively while keeping records of messages sent.
