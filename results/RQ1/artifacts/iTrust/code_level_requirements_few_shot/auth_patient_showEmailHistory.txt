As a patient using this system, you can access a detailed history of your email communications. This feature displays a list of emails that have been sent to you, providing information such as the recipients, subject lines, message contents, and the dates they were sent. You can quickly reference past communications, helping you stay organized and informed about your interactions and any important information you may have received via email. If there have been no emails recorded, the system will inform you that there are currently no emails on record. This helps ensure you have access to your email history in a clear and concise manner.
