As a medical personnel using this system, you can view the historical records of emails that have been sent and received. The system presents these records in the form of a table, displaying details for each email including the recipients, subject, content of the email, and the date it was sent. If no emails are found in the records, the system will inform you that there are no emails on record. This helps you quickly access and track communication history within the medical context for transparency and documentation purposes.
