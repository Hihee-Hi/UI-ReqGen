As a user accessing this system, you will see a header section that displays the application logo and navigational links. If you are logged in with a valid session, your name and a logout option will appear on the top right corner of the page. Your role (such as patient, healthcare provider, or admin) determines the menu options available to you, ensuring that you have access to functionalities pertinent to your role. The header also includes an alert area to display error messages prominently if any issues arise during your interaction with the system. This setup provides a seamless navigational experience tailored to your user profile and current session status.
