As a patient using this system, you would have access to a comprehensive overview of your medical records. You can view your personal information, including contact details and insurance information. The system allows you to review your past office visits, where each visit is detailed with links to complete surveys if they haven't been filled. Additionally, you can explore your family medical history to understand genetic health concerns better. Your allergies and the timeline of when they were discovered can be easily accessed. You have details of the patients you represent through the platform, providing a collaborative view for managing dependents' health records. All basic health history data, including vital statistics like height, weight, and cholesterol levels, are tracked alongside the medical personnel who recorded this information. The system includes records of lab procedures, detailing the tests conducted, their results, and any ongoing procedures with associated commentary. Moreover, it gives you a summary of your immunization history, including any recent vaccinations, with an option to report adverse reactions if they occur within the past year. This holistic and interactive view of your medical history empowers you to stay informed and proactive in your health management.
