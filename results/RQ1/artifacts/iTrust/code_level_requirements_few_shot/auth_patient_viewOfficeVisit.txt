As a patient using this system, you would log in to view detailed information about your past office visits with your healthcare provider (HCP). You can see the date of your visit, the name of the healthcare professional who attended to you, and any notes made during the visit. Additionally, the system displays any diagnoses you received, including their medical codes and descriptions. You can also review the medications prescribed, with detailed information such as usage dates, dosage, and instructions. Lastly, the system shows any procedures you had, listed with their respective codes and descriptions. The system organizes this information in an easy-to-read format, helping you keep track of your medical history and manage your health more effectively.
