As a patient using this system, you can view the healthcare provider (HCP) or physician visits. This system enables you to see a list of the HCPs you have consulted with, alongside their specialties, addresses, and the dates of your office visits. The system provides a checkbox option to declare or undeclare a designated primary HCP. You can select this checkbox to update your designated HCP, which helps in managing your regular and specialized healthcare consultations. Additionally, the system offers a search function allowing you to find HCPs by entering their last name, specialty, or zip code. You can search for new HCPs who are available in your area or specific to your required medical attention. This setup facilitates comprehensive management of provider visits, giving you control over your healthcare journey, and ensuring easy access to needed services.
