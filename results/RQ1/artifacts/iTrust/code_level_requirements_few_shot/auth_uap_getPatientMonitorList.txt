As a User Access Personnel (UAP) using this system, you would:
View a list of patients who have their physiological data monitored. The list provides the names of each patient currently under monitoring.
Select a patient from the list by clicking on their name, which will direct you to another page where you can enter new monitoring data for them.
Each patient in the list is uniquely linked to their monitoring data session, ensuring accurate tracking and updating of their health information.
This system allows you to efficiently access and manage health data for multiple patients, supporting streamlined healthcare monitoring and reporting processes.
