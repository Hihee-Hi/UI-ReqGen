As a healthcare provider using this system, you can: Identify the patient for whom you want to document an office visit. Confirm that you wish to create a new office visit record for the patient by clicking the 'Yes, Document Office Visit' button. This action will generate a new entry for the office visit, which you can later modify with specific details of the visit. In addition to creating new records, you have access to the patient's past office visit records. By clicking on a previous visit date, you can review and update the details for any past visits. This system facilitates efficient management of office visit documentation and ensures that all patient interactions are accurately recorded and easily accessible for future reference.
