As a patient using this system, you would:

Log in to view a list of your upcoming appointments. The system retrieves all scheduled appointments for your account and displays them in a table.

For each appointment, the table provides detailed information, including the healthcare provider's name, the type of appointment, the scheduled date and time, and the duration.

If any appointments overlap or conflict based on scheduling, they are highlighted to draw your attention.

You can access additional information or comments associated with an appointment by clicking on a specific link provided in the table.

If no appointments are scheduled for you, the system informs you that there are no upcoming appointments.

This streamlined view helps you stay informed about your healthcare schedule and proactively manage any conflicts or relevant details for your appointments.
