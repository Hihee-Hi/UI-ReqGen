As a healthcare provider (HCP) using this system, you would:

1. Navigate to the HCP Consultations page to manage your consultations.
2. To send a consultation, select the 'Send a Consultation' option:
   - Choose a patient from your list of associated patients.
   - Choose another HCP to refer the patient to.
   - Provide details about the referral, and submit the form.
   - The system will send the consultation request, and you will receive a confirmation of submission.
3. To view pending consultations, select the 'View Pending Consultations' option:
   - You will see two lists of consultations: outgoing and incoming.
   - You can view the current status (Pending, Finished, or Declined) of each referral, the patient's information, the sender's and receiver's details.
   - You can update a pending consultation referral by clicking the 'edit' option.
4. When updating a consultation:
   - Review and if needed, update the referral and consultation details based on whether you are the sending or receiving HCP.
   - You can change the status of the referral.
   - Submit the updated form to finalize the update and receive a confirmation of the changes made.
5. Manage consultation details, ensuring clear and accurate information is shared while maintaining the confidentiality and proper patient management protocols.

This system allows efficient and secure communication among healthcare providers regarding patient referrals, facilitating better coordination and patient care.
