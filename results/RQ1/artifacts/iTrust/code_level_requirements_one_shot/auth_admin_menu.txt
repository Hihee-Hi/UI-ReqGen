As an administrator utilizing this system, you have several key functionalities at your disposal. Firstly, you can add new entities such as healthcare providers, emergency responders, or public health authorities to the system. For editing-related tasks, you can modify appointment types, manage hospital assignments for healthcare providers, and update various medical code standards including CPT, ND, ICD, and LOINC codes. Furthermore, you have the ability to edit personnel information and update your personal demographic details. In terms of system reminders, you can send appointment reminders and check the reminder message outbox to maintain effective communication with patients. Additionally, you can manage and update hospital listings, adjust global session timeout settings for user sessions, and review patient satisfaction survey results to gauge feedback and improve service delivery. These functions collectively allow you to manage operational aspects, optimize resource allocation, and ensure quality healthcare services within your organization.
