As a healthcare professional using this system, you would:  
1. Select a patient, ensuring you have their unique identifier (PID) before proceeding. If the patient ID is not set, the system redirects you to first specify it.  
2. To document a new office visit for the patient, confirm your action through a single-click interface. After confirmation, the system will create a new office visit record and redirect you to a page where you can edit and input relevant visit details.  
3. Alternatively, you can view a list of past office visits for the patient and select any visit from the list to modify or update its details.  
4. This process streamlines the documentation of office visits, allowing you to focus on either creating new records or managing existing ones for accurate patient care history.
