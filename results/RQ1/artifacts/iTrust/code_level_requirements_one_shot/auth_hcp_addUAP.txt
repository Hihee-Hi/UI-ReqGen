As a healthcare provider adding administrative personnel (UAP) to the system, you would:

Enter the first name, last name, and email address of the new staff member into the provided form. If an email address is unavailable, you can use a pre-defined hospital email for password recovery purposes.

Once you submit the form, the system will create a new record for the staff member. It will assign them a unique Medical ID (MID), generate a temporary password, and classify their role as a UAP.

Afterward, the system will display a confirmation message with the staff’s MID and temporary password. You can pass this information securely to the newly added personnel to allow them access to the system.

Following the successful addition, you would have the option to navigate to another page to further manage or edit the personnel's information.

This process streamlines the addition of UAP staff members and ensures all essential information for their onboarding is created securely and available immediately.
