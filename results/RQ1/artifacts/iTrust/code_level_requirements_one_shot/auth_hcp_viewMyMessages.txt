As a healthcare provider (HCP), to view your messages, you would log into the system and access the page for viewing messages. This page is designed to display all the messages sent to you. If you have any messages, you will see them listed in a table format with details including the sender's name, the content of the message, and the date the message was received. There will also be an option to reply to a message directly by clicking the reply link next to each message. If there are no messages, the page will inform you that there are no messages in your inbox. This streamlined process ensures you can efficiently manage and respond to communications relevant to your medical practice.
