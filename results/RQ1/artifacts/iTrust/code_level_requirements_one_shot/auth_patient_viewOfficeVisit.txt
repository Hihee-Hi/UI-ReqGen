As a patient using this system, you can view the details of a past office visit with your healthcare provider (HCP). Once you access the appropriate section using your login credentials, you will see the date of the visit, the name of the healthcare professional who conducted the visit, and any notes they added about your condition or treatment. Additionally, you’ll find a list of diagnoses made during the visit, each with their corresponding codes and descriptions. Moreover, the system will display any medications prescribed to you, including details such as dosage and usage instructions, along with the period they are intended for use. If there are procedures that were performed, you will also be able to see their codes and descriptions. You can click on the name of your HCP to view more details about them if needed. This functionality allows you to keep track of your medical history and ensure you are informed about your healthcare.
