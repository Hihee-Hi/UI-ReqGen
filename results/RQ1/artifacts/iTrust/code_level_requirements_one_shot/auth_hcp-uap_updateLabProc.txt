As a healthcare provider user of this system, you access the functionality to update laboratory procedures. When you need to change the status, commentary, or results of a lab test, you retrieve the specific procedure using a unique identifier. The system displays the current details of the laboratory procedure, including patient ID, lab code, status, commentary, and results, among others. Upon making the necessary modifications, you submit the updated information through a form. If the updates are processed successfully, you receive a confirmation message. This process allows you to maintain up-to-date and accurate lab records, which is crucial for ensuring high-quality patient care and compliance with healthcare standards.
