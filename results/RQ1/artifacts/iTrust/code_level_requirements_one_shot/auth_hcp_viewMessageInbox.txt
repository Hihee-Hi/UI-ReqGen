As a healthcare professional accessing this system, you can view your message inbox to manage communications related to your healthcare practice. Upon logging in, you can see the messages that have been sent to you. If you have selected a specific message, the system will retrieve and display details about who sent it, the subject, the date and time it was sent, and the body of the message. You also have the option to reply to the message directly. The system ensures that you can view messages easily, providing vital communication features to maintain effective management of patient and colleague interactions within the healthcare environment. Any issues with viewing or accessing messages will redirect you to a safe page to prevent errors.
