As a staff member using this system, you can update your demographic and security information by navigating to the relevant form. To do so:

1. Fill out the form with your updated details, such as your first name, last name, address (including city, state, and zip code), and phone number.
2. Additionally, you can manage your security information by entering a new security question and providing and confirming the corresponding answer.
3. Once completed, submit the form to update your information.
4. If any errors occur during submission (e.g., missing or invalid inputs), you will be notified and can correct the information.
5. After successfully updating, the system will confirm that the changes have been saved.
6. Note that password management is handled separately and can be updated via the "Reset Password" link on the login page.

This feature allows you to maintain accurate and secure personal and professional information within the system.
