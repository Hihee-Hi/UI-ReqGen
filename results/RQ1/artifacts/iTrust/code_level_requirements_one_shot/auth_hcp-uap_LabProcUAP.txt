As a healthcare provider using this system, you will be able to view laboratory procedures associated with a patient. First, you need to provide a patient ID. If you do not have an active patient ID, you will be directed to obtain one. Once you have the patient ID, you can view a list of laboratory procedures for that specific patient. This list includes details such as the patient's name, the lab code for the procedure, access rights, status of the lab procedure, any commentary or notes, results obtained, the associated office visit ID, and the last updated date. If you need to make any changes or updates to a lab procedure, you can click on the "Update" link next to the procedure, which will take you to a page where you can modify the details of that particular lab procedure. This streamlined process helps you efficiently manage and review laboratory data associated with your patients' healthcare records.
