As a user of this system, you would:  
Select a specific personnel record you wish to review from the available list.  
The system will display detailed information about the selected personnel, including their full name, a photo associated with their personnel ID, specialty, office location, contact phone number, and email address.  
This functionality allows you to quickly access and verify professional details for administrative, communication, or collaborative purposes. Since the information is presented in a structured format, it simplifies decision-making processes that rely on personnel data, such as referrals or internal coordination.
