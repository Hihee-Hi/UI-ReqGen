As a healthcare provider using this system, you would navigate to the page to schedule an appointment for a patient. First, ensure that you have selected the correct patient. If not, the system will direct you to a page to confirm or select the correct patient ID. Once the patient is confirmed, you will choose the type of appointment from a list that includes duration details. You will need to select a date and time for the appointment using the available fields for scheduled date, hour, and minutes, as well as specifying AM or PM. Optionally, you can include comments regarding the appointment for personalized notes. After filling out the form correctly, you can submit the appointment request. If everything is appropriately entered, a success message will confirm the scheduling, and you will be redirected to the home page to continue your workflow. If there's an error, such as an unfilled date, appropriate messages will guide you to correct the errors. This process allows you to efficiently handle patient appointments within your practice's scheduling system.
