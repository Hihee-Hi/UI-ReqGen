As a patient using this system, you can report any adverse reactions or issues experienced with prescribed medications by following these steps:

1. Navigate to the page for reporting an adverse event.
2. In the provided text area, describe the incident or reaction, providing as much detail as possible about the adverse event.
3. Submit your report through the "Save" button.

Upon submission:
- If you have multiple linked healthcare providers (HCPs), each HCP who prescribed medication involved in the event will receive a report. You can specify which provider to notify if applicable.
- The system will document the details of the adverse event, including the medication, prescribing provider, and your description.
- Notifications may be sent to healthcare providers to keep them informed and enable follow-up actions.

This process ensures that adverse events are recorded and communicated effectively to the relevant healthcare professionals, helping to address the issue and maintain patient safety.
