As a patient using this system, you will be able to view the details of your scheduled medical appointment. Here's how you can do this:

1. Navigate to the appointment viewing section of your patient portal.
2. If you have a specific appointment in mind you want to view, make sure it is selected.
3. Once the appointment is selected, you will see detailed information about it, including the healthcare provider's name, the type of appointment, the scheduled date and time, and the expected duration.
4. Additional comments related to the appointment will also be displayed, if available.
5. If you happen to select an invalid appointment, the system will redirect you back to the main page listing all your appointments.
6. This review ensures you are well-informed about your upcoming appointments and can plan accordingly.
