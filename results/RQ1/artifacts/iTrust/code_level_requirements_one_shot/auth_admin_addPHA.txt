As an administrator utilizing this system, you would:  
Fill out the form with the personnel's first name, last name, and email address when adding a new Public Health Authority (PHA).  
Submit the information, and the system will automatically create a new personnel profile and assign a unique identifier, known as a Medical ID (MID), to the individual.  
The system will generate a temporary password for the newly added personnel, which you can ensure reaches them for accessing their account securely.  
After the addition, a confirmation message will display the MID and temporary password of the new PHA. You can then proceed to manage or edit their personnel details further on another page.  
This process is streamlined to ensure accuracy in personnel management and immediately enable account creation for Public Health Authorities within the organization.
