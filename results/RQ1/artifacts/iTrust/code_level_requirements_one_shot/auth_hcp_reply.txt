As a healthcare provider, this system allows you to respond to messages received from patients or other colleagues. When replying to a message, you'll view the original sender's details and be prompted to enter a response. You can input a subject line, which will be pre-populated with the original subject prefaced by "RE:", and compose your message in the provided text area. Once you submit your reply, the message will be sent back to the original sender, and you'll be redirected to your message inbox, ensuring efficient communication within the healthcare setting.
