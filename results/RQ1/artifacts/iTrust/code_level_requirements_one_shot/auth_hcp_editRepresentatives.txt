As a healthcare provider managing patient representatives, you would start by retrieving the Patient ID for whom you want to edit representative information. If the ID is not already specified, the system will prompt you to locate the patient ID first. Once you have selected a patient, you may add representatives or remove existing ones. Representatives are responsible for managing certain aspects of the patient’s healthcare information. After making changes, you will receive a confirmation message about the success of your actions. This system enables you to efficiently manage relationships between patients and their representatives, ensuring that the correct individuals have access to the necessary information to assist patients in their care. The list of current representatives is displayed, allowing you to view and manage them with ease, ensuring proper communication and representation arrangements.
