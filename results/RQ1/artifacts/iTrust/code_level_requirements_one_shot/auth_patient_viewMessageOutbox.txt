As a user navigating this system, you can view the details of your sent messages. When accessing this feature, you will be taken to a page that displays the information of a specific message based on its index. The message details include the recipient's name, the subject of the message, and the date and time it was sent. Additionally, you can read the message content itself. If there are any issues with selecting the correct message, such as entering an invalid message index, the system redirects you to an error page or back to the list of sent messages. After reviewing the message, you can easily return to your outbox to look at other messages you have sent.
