As a medical data analyst or healthcare professional using this system, you would view and analyze adverse event reports for a specific medication. The system allows you to select a drug by its identifier and visualizes the occurrence of reported adverse events over several months in a bar chart format. Each bar in the chart represents the number of adverse events reported for each month, allowing you to easily identify trends or patterns in the data. This visualization assists in monitoring drug safety by providing insights into the frequency of adverse reactions, which can be critical for improving patient safety and guiding healthcare decision-making.
