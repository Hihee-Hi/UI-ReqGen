As a healthcare provider, you use this system to send email notifications to patients. If you intend to send an email, you first select the patient by entering their Medical ID (MID). If there's a match, a form opens where you can view your own name in the sender's field and the patient's email and full name in the recipient's field. You determine the purpose of the email by filling out the subject and message body, which already starts with a polite salutation using the patient’s first name. Upon submission, the email is sent to the patient. You'll then see a confirmation displaying the email details and can choose to go back to your main portal page. This process allows you to communicate important reminders or notifications to patients efficiently, ensuring accurate delivery using their registered email information.
