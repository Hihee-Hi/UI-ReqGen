To use this system for authentication, ensure you have proper access to the healthcare backend database. If the system is down or undergoing maintenance, you will be notified that the service is unavailable. When logging in, if your credentials are recognized, you gain access with your user identity confirmed. If you face persistent login issues, the system will halt your session to protect your account, and you may need to restart the authentication process. If you are redirected to an error page during the session, it implies a potential role misalignment, suggesting you attempt the login anew for a correct role assignment.
