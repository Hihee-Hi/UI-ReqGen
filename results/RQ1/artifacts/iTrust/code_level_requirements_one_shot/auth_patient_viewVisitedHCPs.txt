As a patient using this system, you can review a list of healthcare providers (HCPs) you’ve visited. This includes their specialties, locations, and the date of your last visit. You can manage your ongoing relationships with these providers by designating them as your primary HCP by selecting checkboxes next to their names. If you find a provider on the list whom you no longer want as your primary HCP, you can simply uncheck the box to remove them. If you need to find new healthcare providers, you can use the search form to filter providers by their last name, specialty, or zip code. This helps you focus on providers who meet specific criteria important to you, such as those close to your location or specializing in particular medical fields. The system updates your designated HCPs based on your selection, ensuring your healthcare network is personalized to your needs.
