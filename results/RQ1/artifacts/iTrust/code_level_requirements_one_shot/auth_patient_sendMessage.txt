As a patient using this system, you can send messages to your designated healthcare professionals or on behalf of another patient who has authorized you as a representative. To send a message, first, you will select the healthcare professional from a list of your declared healthcare providers. If you are sending on behalf of another patient, you will have the ability to choose from their healthcare providers as well. After selecting the appropriate recipient, you can enter the subject and content of the message. Once completed, submit the form to send the message. This process allows for secure communication between patients and healthcare providers, ensuring that messages are directed correctly and appropriately within the healthcare context.
