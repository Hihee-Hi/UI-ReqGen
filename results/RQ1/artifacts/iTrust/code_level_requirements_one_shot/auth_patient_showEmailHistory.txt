As a patient using this system, you can access a detailed history of all emails sent to you through the platform. When navigating to this section, you’ll see a table listing:

1. The recipients of each email.
2. The subject line of the email.
3. The body or main content of the email.
4. The date and time the email was sent.

If no email records exist, the system will display a message indicating that there are no emails on file. This function helps you keep track of communication and provides easy access to important correspondence related to your healthcare.
