As a healthcare provider (HCP) using this system, you can update a patient's lab procedure information. To do so, you would retrieve the lab procedure details using the patient ID, then update key information such as the status of the lab procedure (e.g., not yet received, pending, completed), the results, and any commentary or notes related to the procedure. After submitting your updates, the system will process and save the new information, confirming a successful update. This ensures that all lab procedures are accurately recorded and accessible for future reference, maintaining accurate medical records for patient care continuity. You can navigate to view all laboratory procedures once the updates are complete.
