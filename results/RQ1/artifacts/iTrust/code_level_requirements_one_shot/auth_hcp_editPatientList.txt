As a healthcare provider using this system, you start by ensuring you have selected a patient using their Medical ID (MID). If not, the system redirects you to a page where you can enter this ID. Once a patient is selected, you can either add or remove them from your monitoring list. Upon submitting your choice, the system confirms whether the patient was successfully added or removed from the list with a message displaying the patient's status change. You also have the option to choose a different patient for editing by clicking a button, which resets the selection, allowing you to start the process with a new MID. This functionality helps in efficiently managing patients under your care by keeping track of those who need regular monitoring.
