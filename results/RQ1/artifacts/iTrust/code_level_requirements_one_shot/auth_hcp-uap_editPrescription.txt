As a healthcare provider using this system, you can view and edit prescription information for your patients during their office visits. If you need to update a patient's prescription, you first select the office visit and prescription you wish to edit. Then, you will be presented with a form where you can modify the dosage and instructions for the medication. After entering the new information, you submit the form to save the changes, and the system confirms the update with a display of the revised prescription details. This process allows you to efficiently ensure that your patients receive accurate and current medication instructions based on their health needs. If any errors occur during the submission, the system will notify you, allowing you to attempt the update again, ensuring a smooth management of patient prescriptions.
