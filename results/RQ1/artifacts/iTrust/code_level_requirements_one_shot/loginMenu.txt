This system allows users, such as healthcare professionals, administrators, or patients, to log in securely by providing their unique identification number (MID) and password. After entering the credentials, users can submit the form to access their personalized account or services. If users forget their password, they have the option to reset it by following a provided link. 

For convenience, the system includes predefined test-login options with preset credentials for different types of user roles, which are only visible in a testing environment and serve to streamline development and debugging processes without exposing sensitive information. Once logged in, the user will be able to navigate their respective functionalities depending on their user role, such as accessing patient data, updating schedules, or performing administrative tasks.
