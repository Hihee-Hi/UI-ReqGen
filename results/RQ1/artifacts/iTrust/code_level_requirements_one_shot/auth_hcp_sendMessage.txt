As a healthcare provider using this system, you can send messages to patients securely. First, ensure that the patient's ID is retrieved for whom you want to send the message. You will be redirected to identify the patient if the ID is not available. Once the patient is identified, you can type the subject and the message body into the designated fields. Upon submission, the message will be sent to the selected patient. The system will notify you of any errors if the information provided is incomplete or incorrect. After sending, the system directs you to the message outbox to manage your messages. This process facilitates easy and quick communication with patients directly within the healthcare management system.
