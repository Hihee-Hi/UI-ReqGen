As a healthcare provider (HCP) using this system, you can view details of a specific appointment. When you select an appointment from your list of appointments, the system will display the patient's name, the type of appointment, the date and time it is scheduled, and the duration of the appointment in minutes. Additionally, any comments associated with the appointment will be displayed. If no comments are present, it will indicate so. This helps you quickly review essential details about the appointments you've scheduled.
