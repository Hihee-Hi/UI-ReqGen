As an administrator using this system, you would access your Reminders Message Outbox to view sent reminder messages. You can sort these messages either by the recipient's name or the time they were sent. When you arrive at this function, a list of all your sent reminder messages will be displayed, and you can apply sorting options to facilitate easier access to specific messages. If you have chosen to sort by name, your messages will be ordered alphabetically based on the recipient's name. If you choose to sort by time, the messages will be ordered according to when they were sent. Additionally, you will have the opportunity to order them in either ascending or descending order according to your preference. From the displayed list, you can see details such as whom the message was sent to, the subject of the message, and the date it was sent. You can choose to view the content of any specific message by selecting the read option associated with it. If there are no messages present, an indication that there are no messages available is displayed. This function helps streamline the management of communication with recipients by offering an organized view of all outgoing reminder messages.
