As a healthcare provider (HCP) using this system, you can manage your list of monitored patients through the following steps: First, ensure you have a specific patient's identification. The system will prompt you to select a patient if it doesn't detect one. Once you have the patient's identification, you'll be able to view their name and determine if they are already on your monitored list. You can then choose to either add them to or remove them from your list. Confirm your action, and the system will update your list accordingly. Afterward, the patient will be added or removed, confirmed through a message, and you will no longer need to select a patient for this interaction again until necessary.
