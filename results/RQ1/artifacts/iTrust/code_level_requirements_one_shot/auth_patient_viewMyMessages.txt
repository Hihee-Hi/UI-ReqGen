As a patient using this system, you can view the messages sent to you by healthcare providers or other authorized personnel. When you access the messages page, you'll see a list of all your messages displayed in a table format. Each message will show the name of the sender, the content of the message, and the date it was received.  

If you wish to respond to a specific message, simply click on the "Reply" link next to that message, and it will take you to another page where you can compose and send your reply.  

If you do not have any messages, the system will display a notification indicating that your inbox is empty. This provides a simple and organized way to manage communication with your healthcare team, ensuring efficient and accessible information exchange.
