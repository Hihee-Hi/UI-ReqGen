This system is utilized in managing user sessions for various roles in a service environment, such as healthcare or administration. As a user:

When you log in, the system automatically identifies your role based on your user type (e.g., patient, healthcare provider, administrator, etc.). It ensures the proper role-based access to system features.

If role identification is missing or inaccessible, the system attempts to detect your role based on predefined permissions associated with patients, healthcare providers, emergency responders, administrators, and other roles.

Should a session become invalid due to technical issues, you may need to re-authenticate to access your account.

This functionality supports secure interactions by dynamically recognizing user permissions and establishing their session context, thereby maintaining system integrity and personalized access to services.
