As a healthcare professional, you can monitor and manage adverse events reported by your patients through this system. When you access the details of a specific adverse event, you are provided with essential information about the report such as the drug involved, the reporting date, and the patient's description of their experience. You have two primary actions you can take: You may choose to remove this event from your records if it's no longer relevant or needed. Alternatively, if you require further insight into the patient's condition or experience, you can initiate a direct communication with the patient to request more information. This interaction allows you to maintain a comprehensive understanding of the patient's adverse reaction to the medication, aiding in patient care and medication safety protocols.
