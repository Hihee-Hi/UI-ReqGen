As a healthcare professional accessing this system, you can efficiently manage and organize your messages. Upon accessing your message inbox, you have the option to sort your messages either by the sender's name or by the time they were received, and you can choose whether to display them in ascending or descending order. You can filter messages based on specific criteria such as the sender, subject, keywords that should or should not be present, and a date range. This filter can be tested before being saved, allowing for customization in viewing important communication. Messages appear in a list format where unread messages are highlighted for easy identification. You can select any message to read its contents. If no messages are available, you'll be informed accordingly. This system streamlines message handling, ensuring effective communication management in your professional environment.
