Upon successfully logging into the system, users are automatically checked for their roles and redirected to their respective home pages, which are personalized based on their role within the organization. These roles can include patient, healthcare provider, user access personnel, emergency responder, public health authority, administrator, or tester. Each user type is directed to an appropriate dashboard that contains specific information and functions related to their role. However, if a valid session is not detected or the user's credentials are not recognized by the system, they are logged out or redirected to an error page informing them that they do not have access to proceed further. This security mechanism ensures that users only access information and functionalities pertinent to their responsibilities.
