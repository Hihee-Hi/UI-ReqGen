As a healthcare provider accessing this system, you can view a list of your patients who have prescription renewal needs. Upon logging in, you will be presented with a table listing patients along with their contact details such as phone number and email address. If there are no patients needing prescription renewals, a message will display indicating no current needs. Each patient's name is clickable, allowing you to directly send an email notification regarding their prescription renewal. This facilitates timely communication and efficient management of patient prescription renewals.
