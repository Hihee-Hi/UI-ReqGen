As a patient using this system, you have a personalized menu that allows you to manage and access various aspects of your healthcare information and interactions. You can update your personal details under "My Demographics" or view logs of who accessed your records in "Access Log". Your medical history is accessible through sections like "My Diagnoses", "My Providers" for healthcare provider interactions, and "View My Records" for comprehensive access to your medical files. You can also review your current and expired prescription data.

Appointments are organized under "View My Appointments", letting you keep track of when and where you need to be for medical consultations. If you need to communicate with healthcare providers, the system offers a "Message Inbox" and "Message Outbox" for secure message exchanges. Telemedicine options are available for remote healthcare monitoring by reporting your health status.

For feedback and broader communication histories, you can review "Satisfaction Survey Results" and check "Show Email History" to revisit correspondence. This structured menu ensures a comprehensive, user-friendly experience tailored to managing your healthcare needs efficiently.
