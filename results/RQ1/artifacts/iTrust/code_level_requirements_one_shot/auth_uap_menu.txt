As a user in the healthcare field, this system enables you to manage various patient-related tasks and administrative functions. You can add new patients, edit personal health records, update patient demographics and health information, and document office visits. You can view laboratory procedures, report requests, and telemedicine status. The system also allows you to assess chronic disease risks and review past email communications and satisfaction survey outcomes. All these activities streamline your interaction with patient information and clinic management, ensuring an efficient and organized workflow within the healthcare environment.
