In this system aimed at remote healthcare monitoring, as a healthcare provider, you will be able to: Access detailed patient information based on remote monitoring data, which includes patient details like name and phone number. Additionally, you can identify patient representatives who act on behalf of the patient, providing any contact information they may have. You can view historical health statistics related to blood pressure and glucose levels for the patient. You can specify a date range to retrieve these monitoring statistics, offering you trend insights across specified timeframes. For health statistics that fall outside typical ranges, visual highlights provide immediate alerting, helping you gauge patient health at a glance. All information is accessible based on the patient's unique identifier from remote monitoring records, ensuring targeted and individualized data access for enhancing patient care delivery.
