As a patient using this system, you can provide feedback on your recent visit through a survey. To complete the survey, first indicate the amount of time you spent waiting in the waiting room and then the time spent in the examination room before seeing your doctor. Next, rate your satisfaction with the office visit overall, choosing from options ranging from very satisfied to very unhappy. Finally, rate your satisfaction with the treatment or information received, again selecting from similar options. After you've filled out the form, submit the survey. Upon successful submission, your feedback will be recorded and you’ll be redirected to your records page. This process allows you to easily report and assess your experiences during office visits.
