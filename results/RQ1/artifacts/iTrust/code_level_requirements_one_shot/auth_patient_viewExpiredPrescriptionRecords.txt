As a patient using this system, you would:

Log in to your account and access the section titled "Get My Expired Prescription Reports."  
Here, you can view a table listing all expired prescriptions associated with your medical record.  
If no expired prescriptions are found, the system will display a message indicating there are no records available.  
When prescriptions are displayed:  
- You can see detailed information for each expired prescription, including the medication's code, description, the start and end dates of the prescription, and the prescribing healthcare provider's name.  
- You can click on the medication's code to access more detailed information about the specific prescription.  
- You can also click on the prescribing healthcare provider's name to view information about the doctor, such as their qualifications or contact details.  

This process enables you to keep track of expired prescriptions and provides contact information for the responsible healthcare providers if further action is needed.
