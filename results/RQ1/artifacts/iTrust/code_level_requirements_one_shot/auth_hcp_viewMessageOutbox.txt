As a healthcare provider using this system, you can view the details of messages you have sent from your outbox. When navigating to this page, the system will display the recipient's name, the subject of the message, the date and time the message was sent, and the body of the message. This allows you to review and confirm the content of your communications with other users. If there was an error or invalid action, you will be redirected back to the outbox or another appropriate page to ensure proper navigation. After reviewing the message, you can click a provided link to return to the outbox for further message management. This process aids in efficient tracking and organization of sent communications while maintaining secure handling of message data.
