As a user of this system, you would:  
Access a list of patients who are being remotely monitored. Upon opening this page, you will see the names of the patients presented in a table.  
If you wish to manage or add telemedicine monitoring data for a specific patient, click on their respective name. By clicking the name, you will be directed to a page where you can input or review detailed monitoring information associated with that patient.  
The system ensures that duplicate entries of patient records are avoided, presenting only unique patient names.  
This process helps healthcare providers optimize their workflow in managing remote patient health data and telemedicine service needs systematically and efficiently.
