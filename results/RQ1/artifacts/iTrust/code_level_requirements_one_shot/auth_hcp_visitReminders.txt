As a healthcare provider using this system, you would interact with the visit reminder functionality by selecting a type of reminder from a dropdown list, which could include categories such as overdue check-ups or upcoming vaccinations. Upon submitting your selection, you would receive a list of patients who require visits based on the reminder type, including their contact information and reasons why a visit is flagged. You have the option to send email notifications directly to the patients by clicking on their names, facilitating communication and appointment scheduling. This feature aims to improve patient follow-up and ensure necessary medical visits occur as intended.
