HCPs can manage lab procedures for office visits by creating, viewing, reassigning, or removing them. When creating a lab procedure, the HCP selects a procedure code, priority (1-3), and Lab Technician, saving the procedure or canceling it. The status is marked as "in transit." The HCP can view the details of a lab procedure, including patient name, procedure code, status, timestamp, and Lab Technician. If the procedure is not yet in the testing state, the HCP can reassign it to another Lab Technician from a list, based on specialty and pending tasks. The reassignment can be confirmed or canceled. Lab procedures can be removed if the status is either "in transit" or "received," with confirmation required. When the status of a lab procedure is updated, the patient is notified with the LOINC number and updated status.
