The iTrust system provides functionality for both patients and LHCPs (Licensed Healthcare Providers) to view prescription lists. A patient or their personal health representative can choose to view a list of prescriptions either for themselves or for a patient they represent. The list is sorted by the prescription's start date, with the most recent prescriptions appearing first. For LHCPs, after selecting a patient from a list of requested reports, they can view the patient’s prescription list, also sorted by start date in descending order.

The prescription report displays the following details: medication name, date prescribed, start date, end date, and the name of the doctor who prescribed the medication. Additionally, an expired prescription report is available, listing prescriptions that have expired. This report is titled with the patient’s name and includes similar information, such as medication name, prescription dates, and the name of the prescribing LHCP, which is linked to the LHCP's contact information. If no expired prescriptions exist, an empty list is displayed.

When a patient clicks on the name of the LHCP for an expired prescription, the contact information for that LHCP is shown. This contact information includes the LHCP’s first and last name, type, street address, city, state, zip code, phone number, and email address. If any contact information is missing, the corresponding field will simply be left blank.
