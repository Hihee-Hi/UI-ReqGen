The patient or their personal health representative can access and view their own personal health information, including historical values, immunizations, and office visit details (date, diagnoses, medications, attending physician name, and lab procedures, but not visit notes). The data is sorted by the most recent access by default, though the patient can choose to view the list sorted by the role of the accessor (personal health representative, LHCP, UAP, Emergency Responder), with each role type sorted by date, most recent first.

The patient or personal health representative can also see an abbreviated health history for immediate family members (siblings, parents, and grandparents) for whom MIDs are available in iTrust. This history includes diagnoses for high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes, cancer, heart disease, smoking history, and cause of death if the family member is deceased. The family health history is displayed in a table with an "x" indicating the diagnosis for each family member.

Additionally, if the patient or personal health representative has not yet completed an office visit satisfaction survey, they have the option to take it; however, once the survey is completed, it cannot be retaken or viewed again.
