The iTrust system provides users (patients or LHCPs) with a calendar view of their appointments for the current month. The calendar displays the user’s appointments, office visit dates, prescription dates, and lab procedure dates. Each date entry is labeled with relevant details: office visits show diagnoses (ICD-9CM codes), prescriptions show prescribed medications (NDC codes), and lab procedures show the corresponding LOINC codes. Users can click on any entry to view further details about the appointment, office visit, prescription, or lab procedure. Appointment details include the type, date, time, and the name of the associated healthcare provider or patient. Office visit details show diagnoses, procedures performed, lab procedures ordered, prescribed medications, and the name of the prescribing doctor. Prescription details provide the medication names, dates prescribed, start and end dates, and the prescribing doctor. Laboratory procedure details include procedure codes, status, results, and the office visit date when the procedure was ordered. Conflicting appointments (those with overlapping times) are highlighted in bold.
