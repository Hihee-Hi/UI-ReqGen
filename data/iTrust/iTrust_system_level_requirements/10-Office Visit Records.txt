Healthcare Providers (HCPs) can modify or delete details of an office visit, including the date, hospital, notes, prescriptions, lab procedures, referrals, diagnoses, procedures, and immunizations. All changes are logged, and the HCP can review the modifications in the office visit record. Additionally, Licensed Healthcare Providers (LHCPs) can view a list of all patients they've seen, with details such as the patient’s name, address, and the date of the last office visit. The list is sorted by the most recent visit.
