The iTrust system enables messaging between patients (or their representatives) and LHCPs (Licensed Healthcare Providers), allowing them to send and receive messages with detailed features for managing communication. A patient or representative can send a message to an LHCP by selecting from a list of DLHCPs, entering a subject (up to 100 characters) and a message (up to 1000 characters), and clicking "send." The message appears in the patient’s outbox and the LHCP’s inbox, with a fake email notification sent to the LHCP about the new message. Similarly, LHCPs can send messages to patients or representatives, with the message appearing in their outbox and in the patient’s inbox, along with an email notification to the patient/representative.

Both patients/representatives and LHCPs can reply to received messages by selecting the "reply" link, entering their response, and sending it. A "RE:" prefix is added to the subject line for replies. After sending, the message appears in the sender’s outbox and the recipient’s inbox, with email notifications sent to both parties.

Messages can be read by opening the inbox or outbox, with unread messages bolded for easy identification. Users can view detailed message content by clicking the "Read" link beside the message row. Messages in both the inbox and outbox can be sorted by either sender/recipient or timestamp, in ascending or descending order, but this sorting is not saved across sessions.

The system also provides advanced filtering options for users to refine their message display, based on criteria like sender, subject, specific words in the message, or a date range. Users can modify the filter, test the search, or save the changes, and only one filter is saved per user across sessions. The filter is populated with values from the last saved configuration when accessed again.
